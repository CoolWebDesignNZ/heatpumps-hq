## Is it time for a heat pump replacement?

An old heat pump doesn't need replacing just because it's old. How it's working matters more than its age.

- **It can't heat the room on frosty mornings.** Even after a service and with clean filters.
- **The same fault keeps coming back.** Or a big part like the compressor has failed.
- **It runs on an older refrigerant.** That gas is getting hard and costly to get for repairs.
- **The power bill has crept up.** And you haven't changed how you use it.
- **The house has changed.** A renovation, extension or new insulation can mean the room needs a different size of unit.

If only one of these fits, a repair or service may be enough. See how we weigh up [heat pump repair](/heat-pump-repair/) against replacing.

## Heat pump replacement in Christchurch homes

Lots of Christchurch houses have changed since their old heat pump went in. That changes what the new one should be.

- **Rebuilt and repaired homes.** In [Dallington](/dallington/), some houses were rebuilt after the earthquakes and others were repaired. Repair work sometimes disturbed the insulation, and it wasn't always put back. We check what the work left behind before we size the new unit, because the old size may now be wrong.
- **Frosty mornings on the plains.** Out in [Templeton](/templeton/), clear nights bring frost, and the heat pump defrosts its outdoor coil on those mornings. If your old unit can't keep up then, we size the new one for the coldest mornings, not an average day.
- **Salt by the sea.** On the dune streets of [North New Brighton](/north-new-brighton/), salt makes the coil fins and casing corrode faster. Rust spots, a white crust on the fins or weaker heating mean it's time for a check. If the unit is past fixing, the new one goes on the side of the house sheltered from the easterly.
- **Units in the wrong spot.** In [Spreydon](/spreydon/), some older outdoor units went in a corner or right under a neighbour's bedroom. A replacement is the time to move it, so it runs quieter and works better.
- **Heat pumps that came with the house.** Plenty of [Aidanfield](/aidanfield/) homes came with one, and it may be getting on. Weaker heat on cold mornings or louder running? We check it first. Sometimes a service or repair brings it back.

## Heat pump upgrades short of replacement

Not every problem needs a new heat pump. Before we quote a replacement, we check whether one of these would fix it.

- **A full service.** A dirty indoor coil or fan cuts output. So does a blocked outdoor coil or a slow gas leak. A [service](/heat-pump-servicing/) gets a sound unit back to its best.
- **Moving the outdoor unit.** Boxed in by a fence or a tight cover, it breathes its own cold air back in. Out in the open, it can work a lot better.
- **Better controls.** A [Wi-Fi controller](/smart-heat-pump-systems/) lets you run proper timers and schedules. No more leaving it on all day, or blasting it on high when the house is cold.
- **Adding a unit.** Is one heat pump trying to heat a whole open plan house? A second, smaller unit at the far end may beat one bigger replacement. See [multi-room installation](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/).

## How we replace an old heat pump

1. **We look at the old system and the house.** The rooms, the insulation, the windows and how you use the space. The old unit's size is a starting point, not the answer. The house may have changed since it went in. So may the way you use it.
2. **You get options in the quote.** Usually one in the same spot. If it helps, one in a better spot too. For each model we show you the Cold zone heating stars and the heating output at 2°C from the energy label. That way you can compare how well they run.
3. **We take out the old unit.** We recover the refrigerant before it leaves your property.
4. **We fit the new system.** Brackets or feet, pipework and drainage, all tidy. We only reuse old pipework if it suits the new unit and it's in good nick. If not, we replace it.
5. **A licensed electrical worker does the wiring.** Electricians must give you a Certificate of Compliance for fixed wiring work. Keep it with your house records.
6. **We test it.** We run it in heating and cooling. Then we show you how the controls work.

## Comparing old and new heat pump efficiency

The energy label changed in 2021. The stars on the old Energy Rating Label measured something different. You can't compare them with the stars on today's Zoned Energy Rating Label. So we don't promise a percentage saving. What we can tell you is how the new unit's rated output matches what your room needs, and how it rates against other models the same size. To learn each part of the label, see [reading the energy rating label](/energy-star-heat-pumps/).
