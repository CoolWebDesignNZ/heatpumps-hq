## Heating a Tai Tapu home, farmhouse or new build

Tai Tapu is a small village in Selwyn District. Lifestyle blocks, horse properties and farms surround it. Most homes here are bigger than a town house. They have more rooms and longer halls. Some have a sleepout, studio or granny flat off on its own. Two things decide the heat pump: how old the house is, and how far its rooms spread.

- **Older farmhouses:** these often have high ceilings, single glazing and separate rooms off long halls. Warm air collects up under a high ceiling. It stops at every shut door. One lounge unit leaves the bedrooms and kitchen cold. These houses usually need a unit in each room you use. Check the ceiling insulation before we size them. A cold roof space means every unit has to be bigger.
- **Big modern homes on a few hectares:** they hold heat far better. Their length is the problem. A [ducted system](/ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/) with a vent in each room suits them if the roof space is clear. If it isn't, a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/) does the job.
- **Sleepouts, studios and granny flats:** if one sits away from the house, it's usually too far for a long pipe run from the main system. So it gets its own small indoor and outdoor unit.
- **Homes on the lower slopes:** on the southern side, where the Port Hills meet the plains, the hill can take the winter sun earlier in the day. Rooms facing the slope stay cooler, and we size their units to match. Houses on the flat get harder frosts. More on that below.

## Replacing a Tai Tapu log burner with a heat pump

Swapping a log burner for heat pumps? Most Tai Tapu owners want every room warm, with no wood to stack. Older country houses were often built round a fire in the lounge. The rest of the house was left cold. In a big house with doors between rooms, one large lounge unit won't cut it. Here's what usually works:

- **A ducted system:** if the roof space allows. Vents in each room, and one controller.
- **Several wall or floor console units:** sized room by room, on one or more outdoor units.
- **A unit for the outbuilding:** so a sleepout or office has its own heat.

We size each system on what we find in your house. That means room sizes, insulation and glazing, and which rooms you use most.

## Frost on low ground around Tai Tapu

Cold air runs downhill and settles on the flat. So low spots near Tai Tapu can get heavy frosts on still nights. We put the outdoor unit up on raised feet or a stand. Melt water from defrosting runs away, and air flows freely underneath.

On a big section, keep it clear of long grass, garden beds and anything else that could block it. Older farmhouses often have high ceilings. There, a [floor console](/single-room-heat-pump-installation/) puts warm air down where you sit.
