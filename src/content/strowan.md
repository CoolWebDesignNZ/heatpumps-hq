## Choosing heat pumps for Strowan homes

Strowan sits just north west of the city centre, between Merivale and Papanui. Most of the suburb was built up in the early and mid twentieth century, and a lot of that housing is still standing. That shapes the work.

- **Villas and bungalows.** Timber framed, timber floors, often single glazed. Many have had insulation added under the floor and in the ceiling since, but the walls are usually still uninsulated. These houses lose heat faster than their floor area suggests, so the unit needs more capacity than a newer house of the same size.
- **State houses and 1950s to 1970s homes.** Solid and simple in layout, with separate living rooms and bedrooms off a hallway. One unit in the hall rarely heats the rooms around it well. A multi-room or ducted system usually suits better.
- **Rebuilt and newer homes.** A number of sections have been subdivided and rebuilt. These hold heat well and often need only one high wall unit in the living area.

The suburb is flat, so access is straightforward. The main constraint is usually where the outdoor unit can sit without crowding a boundary or blocking a path.

## Heat pump installation in Strowan's older houses

The job on an older Strowan house is getting the heat where it's needed. A single unit at one end of a long living room leaves the far end cold. We look at the room shape, the window area and the ceiling height before we recommend a position.

Two things come up often here:

- **Timber floors.** They're cold underfoot and they leak heat downwards. Underfloor insulation makes a real difference, and it can mean a smaller unit. We check what's there at the quote.
- **Single glazing.** Large original windows lose heat quickly at night. Heavy curtains help. A unit sized for the daytime heat load will cope at night if the curtains are drawn.

If the house has been insulated and draught proofed, say so when you book. It changes the sizing.

## Servicing heat pumps in Strowan

An older house works a heat pump harder. More heat loss means the unit runs longer each day, and a unit that runs longer collects more dust. A yearly [heat pump service](/heat-pump-servicing/) keeps it efficient.

The service covers the filters, the indoor coil, the condensate drain and the outdoor unit. On an older system we also check the refrigerant charge and listen for bearing noise in the fan. Catching those early is cheaper than a repair later. If your unit is more than a few years old and hasn't been serviced, a [seasonal tune-up](/heat-pump-seasonal-tune-ups/) is a sensible start.

## Heat pump repair in Strowan

Heat pumps fail in fairly predictable ways. The unit stops heating, blows cold air, drips water, or makes a noise it didn't before. Some of these are simple: a blocked filter, a drain full of algae, a thermostat out of calibration. Others need parts.

We repair all the major brands, including Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, Fujitsu, Panasonic and Toshiba. If you can tell us the model and what it's doing when you ring, we'll bring the likely parts on the first visit. For a unit that has stopped altogether in cold weather, see [emergency heat pump repair](/emergency-heat-pump-repair/).
