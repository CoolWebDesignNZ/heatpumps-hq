## Heat pumps for Moncks Bay homes by the water

In Moncks Bay, two things decide the heat pump. Which side of the coast road is the house on? And how much salt is in the air? Moncks Bay is a small stretch of the estuary edge, between Redcliffs and the foot of Mount Pleasant. Every address is only metres from the water.

- **Houses facing the water.** Across the coast road, they take the wind straight off the estuary. A house in the wind loses heat faster. We size for a windy winter evening. The outdoor unit goes on the sheltered side of the house.
- **Houses set into the slope.** These sections are cut into the hill, with retaining walls and steps up to the front door. Rooms that back onto a retaining wall get little sun and can feel damp. They often need their own small unit. On a mild, damp day, dry mode helps a lot.
- **Rebuilt and strengthened homes.** There are many since the earthquakes, and they usually hold heat well. A modern rebuild may need just one high wall unit in the main living area, sized right. Add a small unit for bedrooms at the cold end of the house.

## Heat pump installation in Moncks Bay waterfront homes

A house facing the bay is open to wind and spray off the water. The outdoor unit is best on the far side of the house, or behind a solid fence. Don't put it on the front deck looking at the view. Tucked away, it misses the worst of the salt. It also won't be the first thing you see and hear when you sit outside.

The coil fins on the outdoor unit are thin aluminium. Salt deposits shorten their life. Three things help. Give it a gentle hose rinse every couple of months. Check the casing for rust spots. And get it serviced every year.

## Moncks Bay homes cut into the slope

Behind the road, space is the problem. A section cut into the hill may leave only a narrow gap between the house and a retaining wall. The outdoor unit needs clear space around it to breathe. So a wall bracket higher up is sometimes the better choice. The drain line from the indoor unit needs a steady fall. That can mean taking it to a downpipe or drain, so it doesn't run onto the path.

## Cleaning heat pump filters in Moncks Bay

Clean your indoor filters every few weeks in winter. Vacuum them, or rinse them in lukewarm water. Let them dry before you put them back. They fill with dust and lint here, the same as anywhere. With clogged filters, a unit works harder and heats less. Our [maintenance and servicing](/maintenance-servicing/) visits also cover the coil, fan and drain.
