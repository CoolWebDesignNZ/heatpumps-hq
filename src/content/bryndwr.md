## Heat pumps for Bryndwr's mid-century bungalows

Bryndwr sits between Fendalton and Papanui. It's quiet streets of mid-century bungalows on good sized sections, with big trees and old gardens. Most share one plan. The lounge and kitchen are at the front or on the sunny side. A hallway runs down the middle, with bedrooms off it. That plan decides the heat pump.

- **The sunny front rooms.** They soak up heat in the day. So their unit does most of its work in the evening and on dull days. A high wall unit sized for the lounge and kitchen is usually right.
- **Bedrooms across the hall.** They get the least sun and the least heat from the lounge. They're the coldest rooms at night. Each needs its own small unit, or a head on a multi-room system.
- **Rooms under big trees.** They need more heat than the same room with open sky.
- **Ceilings and floors.** Bungalows this age often have gaps in the insulation. We check before we size anything.

## Heat pump installation for a Bryndwr hallway plan

The hallway is the problem. Warm air from the lounge has to get round corners and through doors to reach the bedrooms. It rarely does. With one heat pump, the lounge is warm and the far bedroom is freezing. Here are the fixes, simplest first.

1. **Put the lounge unit in the right spot.** Fit it at the hallway end of the room, blowing along the room. That pushes the most warmth into the rest of the house.
2. **Add bedroom units.** A small wall unit in each bedroom you use. Or a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/) on one outdoor unit.
3. **Go ducted.** A [ducted heat pump](/ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/) sits in the roof space. It sends warm air to the hallway and each bedroom through ceiling vents.

When we quote, we measure the rooms and check the insulation and windows. Then we price each option that suits the house. We show you which rooms each one heats.

## Insulation in older Bryndwr bungalows

Like most mid-century houses, Bryndwr bungalows often went up with little or no insulation. Some still have gaps in the ceiling or under the floor. Fill those and the rooms lose less heat. The heat pump runs at a lower output and costs less to run. We look in the ceiling when we come to quote. We'll tell you what we find.

## Bryndwr gardens and the outdoor unit

Big gardens are a big part of Bryndwr. They matter for the outdoor unit. It needs open air all round. Keep it clear of shrubs that grow in on it. Keep it away from where leaves pile up in autumn. A wall bracket or a raised pad keeps it off damp ground. Shade from big trees also makes some rooms colder. We allow for that when we size.
