## Choosing heat pumps for Hillsborough homes

Hillsborough sits on the hills and in the valley on the south side of Christchurch, above the Heathcote. The suburb runs from sloping streets up towards the hill reserves down to flatter ground near the river. That mix of levels shapes what a heat pump has to do, and where the outdoor unit can go.

- **Older hillside houses.** Many of the homes on the slopes are older, with timber floors, single glazing and rooms that face the weather. They lose heat faster than their floor area suggests, so they need more capacity than a newer house of the same size.
- **Homes on the flat near the valley.** Down on the flatter ground, sections are more level and the outdoor unit is easier to place. These houses still feel the cold easterly and the frost that settles in the valley on clear winter nights.
- **Rebuilt and newer homes.** Some houses here were rebuilt or renovated after the earthquakes, and most are well insulated. In those, one high wall unit often heats an open plan living area without trouble.

## Heat pump installation in Hillsborough

Getting the install right starts with sizing. We measure the rooms, look at the insulation and glazing, and match the unit to how the house actually holds heat. On the hills that often means a slightly larger unit than the floor area alone would suggest. [Heat pump sizing](/heat-pump-sizing/) explains what we check.

The outdoor unit is the other half of the job. On a sloping section it usually goes on a wall bracket or a deck, clear of the ground and out of the prevailing wind. It has to keep good airflow and sit where the pipes can reach the indoor unit without a long, awkward route. We plan all of that at the quote visit so it is in the quote.

For a single room, a [single room install](/single-room-heat-pump-installation/) is often enough. For an open plan living and dining area, a [high wall unit](/high-wall-heat-pumps/) is the common choice. If you want several rooms heated from one outdoor unit, a [multi room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/) is worth a look.

## Servicing heat pumps in Hillsborough

A heat pump that runs hard through winter needs a yearly service. We clean the filters and coil, check the drainage and look for wear before it becomes a breakdown. In a home with pets or on a dusty section, the filters clog faster and want checking more often.

## Cold snaps, frost and the valley

The valley floor in this part of Christchurch gets frost on clear winter nights, and the easterly can push damp air up against the hills. A heat pump sized for a mild day will struggle on the coldest mornings. That is why we size for the cold end of the year, not the average.

If your house feels draughty or the unit seems to run constantly, [winterising the heat pump](/winterisation-of-heat-pumps/) and checking the insulation can make a real difference. A well set up unit holds a steady temperature with less effort.
