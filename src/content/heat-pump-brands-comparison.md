## What actually differs between models

Whatever the brand, these are the things we compare when we pick units for a house.

- **Heating output at 2°C and below.** Some models hold their output in the cold better than others. The [energy label](/energy-star-heat-pumps/) shows heating output at 2°C, so you can check it.
- **Cold zone heating stars and the estimated kWh per year.** They show how efficient a model is in the cold and roughly how much power it will use in a year.
- **Noise figures.** Indoor and outdoor, where the label shows them. They matter most in a bedroom, or where the outdoor unit sits near a neighbour.
- **The fit.** The unit's size, where the pipes connect, and whether a console or slim ducted unit fits the space. A good model is no use if it can't go where you need it.
- **Controls.** Some have Wi-Fi built in, others need an adaptor, and the apps work differently. You'll use the controls every day.
- **Coastal protection.** Homes near the sea need it on the outdoor unit.
- **Parts and service in Christchurch.** You want parts and repairs on hand for the years after the install.

## Heat pump brands for Christchurch homes

We pick models for the house in front of us. These local conditions narrow the choice.

- **Salt and wind on the coast.** In [Clifton](/clifton/), wind off the sea drives salt onto the coil. We check each maker's coastal advice for the models we quote, and look for a spot in the lee of the house.
- **Frost and fog by the estuary.** In [Bexley](/bexley/), cold, still nights bring frost and fog, and that's when the outdoor unit defrosts most. Some units keep their output in the cold better than others, so we compare the 2°C heating figure model by model.
- **Renovations with the ceiling open.** In [Somerfield](/somerfield/), a renovation where the ceiling's coming down anyway is a good time for ducted. Where the ceiling space is tight, a slim ducted unit can help, so we measure the space and compare the slim models side by side.
- **Villa lounges with low windows.** In [Riccarton](/heat-pumps-riccarton/) villas, a floor console can suit a lounge with a low window. All four brands list floor consoles, so it comes down to the model that fits under the sill.
