## Why Heat Pumps HQ started

Heat Pumps HQ started because Nate Thompson kept seeing homeowners leave with heat pumps that were more powerful and more expensive than their houses called for. He saw it during years working for a larger heating company, and founded Heat Pumps HQ in Christchurch in 2015.

A unit that is too big costs more to buy and does not make a room any warmer. It tends to switch on and off in short bursts instead of running steadily. Nate set up his own company to quote the system a house actually needs, and he still owns and runs it.

## How we work at Heat Pumps HQ

Three rules shape every job we take on.

**Sizing starts with the house.** Before we recommend anything, we look at the rooms you want heated, how well the ceiling, walls and floor are insulated, the size and direction of the windows, and how the house is used through the day. A sunny, well insulated lounge needs a very different unit from a cold back bedroom with single glazing. The method we use is set out in [heat pump sizing](/heat-pump-sizing/).

**The price is fixed first.** Quotes are free. Once we know what the job involves, you get a fixed price in writing, and that is the price you pay for the work quoted. If we find a repair makes more sense than a new unit, we say so.

**The electrical side is done properly.** A licensed electrical worker makes the electrical connection on every install.

## The brands we install and service

We install four brands: **Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, Fujitsu and Toshiba**. With four brands to choose from, we compare their models for your rooms and recommend the one that fits, rather than whatever happens to be in stock.

For servicing and repairs we work on those four plus **Panasonic**, and on most other split and ducted heat pumps sold in New Zealand. See [heat pump servicing](/maintenance-servicing/) and [heat pump repairs](/heat-pump-repair/).
