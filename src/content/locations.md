## Heat pumps near you, from the city to Selwyn

We work right across Christchurch, out to Lyttelton and the Selwyn towns.

What suits your home depends a lot on where you live. Older villas lose heat through the floor. Hill homes often need the outdoor unit on a bracket. Close to the beach, the unit needs a spot out of the salt wind.

Wherever you are, we fit Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, Fujitsu and Toshiba, fix and service all the main brands, and give you a fixed price before we start.

## Heat pumps in the Central City and inner suburbs

Close to town, sections are small and neighbours are close. Older villas and cottages lose heat through the floor and single glazing, so they often need a unit in each room you use. Newer townhouses are better insulated but have tight side paths, which makes the outdoor unit's spot and its noise at the boundary the main job. In a Central City apartment, the body corporate usually has to approve the outdoor unit first.
