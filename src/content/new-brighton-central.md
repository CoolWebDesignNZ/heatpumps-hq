## Heat pump installation around the New Brighton Central shops

Near the New Brighton shops, the first question is simple. Does your home stand on its own, or share a wall? Older houses sit next to blocks of flats. There are units built behind the original homes, and newer townhouses. Sections are smaller than in the rest of the area.

- **Standalone older houses.** They lose heat through the roof, walls and windows on every side. We size the unit for that. It's worth checking the ceiling insulation first.
- **Flats and units.** They share at least one wall. A small high wall unit often heats the living area. The harder question is where the outdoor unit can go. And how close it ends up to someone else's bedroom.
- **Newer townhouses.** They're usually better insulated than the houses around them. But they're close to the neighbours too. So noise and the pipe run decide where the outdoor unit goes.

## Sizing heat pumps for New Brighton Central flats

A flat with neighbours on both sides loses less heat than a standalone house the same size. The shared walls stay warm. So a smaller heat pump often does the job. Upstairs units can pick up heat from below. Top floor units lose more through the roof. We size every job from your actual rooms and how the place is built. A compact flat shouldn't be sold a unit meant for a family house. For one living space, see [single-room installation](/single-room-heat-pump-installation/).

## Fitting outdoor units on tight sites

With little or no yard, the outdoor unit needs more thought than the indoor one:

- **Wall brackets.** High enough to keep the unit off the ground and clear of paths.
- **Balconies and decks.** Only if the unit can breathe and drain properly.
- **The pipe run.** Back-to-back is neatest, with the outdoor unit right behind the indoor one. It's not always possible on a shared wall.
- **Noise.** With neighbours close by, the unit has to sit away from their bedroom windows. Our [boundary guide](/heat-pump-boundary-rules-consent-nz/) explains the rules.

In a body corporate? Get approval before you book. We can give you the unit's size, position and pipe route for the application.

## Rental homes near the town centre

Do you rent out a house or flat near the town centre? Under the Healthy Homes heating standard, it needs fixed heating that can heat the main living room directly. A heat pump with a thermostat is one of the accepted options. We size it from the room, not just to the minimum.
