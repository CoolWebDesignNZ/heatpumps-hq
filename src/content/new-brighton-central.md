## Heat pump installation around the New Brighton Central shops

Near the New Brighton shops, the first thing that shapes a heat pump install is whether your home stands on its own or shares a wall. Older houses sit beside blocks of flats, units built behind original homes and newer townhouses, and sections are smaller than in the rest of the area.

- **Standalone older houses** lose heat through the roof, walls and windows on every side, so the unit is sized for that heat loss, and the ceiling insulation is worth checking first.
- **Flats and units** share at least one wall with a neighbour and lose less heat, so a small high wall unit often heats the living area. The harder question is where the outdoor unit can go and how close it ends up to someone else's bedroom.
- **Newer townhouses** are usually better insulated than the houses around them, but they sit close to their neighbours too, so noise and the pipe run decide the outdoor position.

## Sizing heat pumps for New Brighton Central flats

A flat with neighbours on both sides loses less heat than a standalone house of the same size, because the shared walls stay warm. That means a smaller heat pump often does the job. Upstairs units can gain heat from below, and top floor units lose more through the roof. We size every job from the actual rooms and how the home is built, so a compact flat isn't sold a unit meant for a family house. For a single living space, see [single-room installation](/single-room-heat-pump-installation/).

## Fitting outdoor units on tight sites

Where there's little or no yard, we look at:

- **Wall brackets** high enough to keep the unit off the ground and clear of paths.
- **Balconies and decks**, if the unit can breathe and drain properly.
- **The pipe run.** Back-to-back installs, with the outdoor unit directly behind the indoor one, are neatest, but not always possible on a shared wall.
- **Noise.** With neighbours close by, the unit needs to sit away from their bedroom windows. The rules are explained in our [boundary guide](/heat-pump-boundary-rules-consent-nz/).

If you're in a body corporate, get approval before booking. We can supply the unit's size, position and pipe route for the application.

## Rental homes near the town centre

If you let a house or flat near the town centre, the [Healthy Homes heating standard](https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/healthy-homes/heating-standard/) means it needs fixed heating that can directly heat the main living room. A heat pump with a thermostat is one of the accepted options. We size it from the room, not just from the minimum.
