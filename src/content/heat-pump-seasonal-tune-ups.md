## Why a pre-season heat pump check up

A heat pump can sit unused for months and still look fine. Its first real test is the first cold snap. That's when problems show up. A weak capacitor. A sticky louvre motor. A blocked defrost drain. A tune-up moves that test to a day that suits you.

## What a heat pump tune-up checks

We check output and start-up. We check it's ready to defrost or drain. Then the filters, settings and sensors.

**Heating or cooling output.** We run it in the mode you're about to rely on. We measure the air going into and out of the indoor unit. If it's weak, we look further at airflow or refrigerant. Before winter, not in the middle of it.

**Start-up.** We watch the outdoor unit start, speed up and settle. Hesitating, cycling on and off, or a loud start are early warnings.

**Ready to defrost (before winter).** We look at the outdoor unit's base, its drain holes and the ground under it. On a Canterbury frost night it runs defrost cycles to melt ice off the coil. That water needs somewhere to go.

**Drain (before summer).** Cooling and dry mode put water through the drain line all day. We make sure it runs clear.

**Filters.** Washed and put back. That way the check shows how the unit should run.

**Remote, timer and settings.** Batteries, clock, timer and mode. A unit left alone all off season is still on whatever you set last.

**Sensors.** Room never feels like the number on the remote? We check where the unit reads the temperature from. If it's still off, see [thermostat calibration](/heat-pump-thermostat-calibration/).

## Tune-up or full service?

A tune-up is about being ready. A full [heat pump service](/heat-pump-servicing/) is about how clean it is and what shape it's in. The coil, fan wheel, drain tray and outdoor coil all get a proper clean.

- **Serviced in the last year?** A tune-up is usually enough.
- **Not serviced for a year or more?** Get the full service. It covers everything in a tune-up.
- **A smell, a drip or dust on the wall?** You need a clean. See [heat pump cleaning](/heat-pump-cleaning/).

When you book, we'll tell you which one your unit needs. The price is agreed up front.

## Seasonal heat pump maintenance in autumn and spring

Autumn gets it ready for heating. Spring gets it ready for cooling.

**Autumn, for heating.** If you only do one check a year, do this one. Book once the summer heat has gone but before the frosts. That leaves time to sort anything we find.

**Spring, for cooling.** Worth it if you cool bedrooms in summer. Or have a north-facing living area. Or use dry mode on muggy days. We focus on the drain, the coil and the cooling output.

Want both done every year without having to remember? We can put them on a [service plan](/customised-heat-pump-service-plans/).

## What you can check yourself first

Four quick checks before you book:

- Run it for twenty minutes in the mode you'll need. Feel the airflow.
- Wash the filters.
- Put new batteries in the remote. Set the clock.
- Clear anything from round the outdoor unit.

Airflow feels weak? Takes ages to warm the room? Book the tune-up now. Don't wait for winter.
