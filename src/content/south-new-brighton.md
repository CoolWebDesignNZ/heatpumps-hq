## Heat pump installation on both sides of South New Brighton

South New Brighton is a narrow stretch of land, with the beach on one side and the Avon Heathcote Estuary on the other. Homes on the beach side take sea spray and the full easterly. Homes on the estuary side are more sheltered from spray, but the air off the water is often damp, and low-lying sections can stay wet through winter.

So the install depends on which side of the suburb you're on. A beach-side home needs the outdoor unit tucked away from the sea wind. An estuary-side home needs the unit kept well clear of wet ground and the indoor unit placed to deal with damp.

## Damp homes near the estuary

A cold house near the estuary often shows it in the windows: condensation in the morning, mould in the corners of bedrooms. Keeping rooms at a steady temperature is the best thing a heat pump does here, because warm surfaces don't collect as much moisture. Running it on a low, constant setting through winter usually works better than heating hard for an hour in the evening.

Where damp is a problem, we look at the bedrooms too. A small unit in the main bedroom, or a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/), stops the back of the house turning cold and wet overnight.

## Soft ground and outdoor units in South New Brighton

Much of South New Brighton is sand, and some areas settled after the earthquakes. A slab dropped onto loose ground can sink at one end, which tips the unit and stops it draining defrost water. We either build a firm level base or mount the unit on a wall bracket so it doesn't rely on the ground at all. Either way, it sits high enough to stay out of puddles.
