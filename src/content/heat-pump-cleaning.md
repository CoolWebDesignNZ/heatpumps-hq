## What gets dirty, and where

Air is drawn into the top of the indoor unit, through the filters, across the cold or hot coil, and pushed out by a long barrel-shaped fan. Each stage collects something different:

- **Filters** catch hair, lint and coarse dust. They're the part you see.
- **The coil** catches fine dust that slips past the filters. In cooling and dry mode it's wet, and wet dust grows mould.
- **The fan wheel** picks up a sticky grey or black coating on each blade. It flings bits of it out of the unit, which is where the specks on the louvres and the wall come from.
- **The drain tray** under the coil holds a film of slime that can block the drain.

A filter clean only deals with the first one.

## How to clean your heat pump filters

1. Turn the unit off with the remote, then at the isolator if you can reach it.
2. Lift the front panel until it clicks open.
3. Slide the filters down and out.
4. Vacuum the dust off, then rinse them from the back under a lukewarm tap. A drop of mild dishwashing liquid helps with grease.
5. Shake off the water and let them dry in the shade. Hot sun can warp them.
6. Slide them back in the right way round and close the panel.

Do this every few weeks while the unit is in daily use. Some units have extra filters clipped behind the main ones; your manual says whether those can be washed or need replacing.

## What a professional clean involves

**The unit is protected.** We fit a waterproof cleaning bag around the indoor unit to collect the run-off, and cover the floor and furniture below.

**The coil is cleaned and rinsed.** A coil cleaner goes onto the fins, front and back, and is rinsed through with clean water so nothing is left to feed new growth.

**The fan wheel is cleaned.** The blades are washed until the coating comes off. On some models the fan can be cleaned in place; on others it needs more of the unit opened up.

**The drain tray and line are flushed.** Slime is cleared and water is run through to prove the drain is free.

**The louvres and casing are washed.** Then the filters go back in and the unit runs to dry the coil.

**The outdoor unit is cleaned.** Leaves and lint come off the fins and the base is cleared so defrost water drains.

A full clean takes longer than a standard [service](/heat-pump-servicing/), because the indoor unit is properly wet-cleaned rather than inspected and wiped.

## When to book a clean

- The unit smells when it starts. Our [heat pump smells](/heat-pump-deodorising/) page helps tell a dirt smell from a fault.
- You can see black spots inside the outlet when the louvres open.
- There are grey marks on the wall or ceiling above the unit.
- Someone in the house has allergies or asthma and the unit hasn't been cleaned in a while.
- You've moved into a house and don't know when the heat pump was last cleaned.
- The unit has cooled through a long summer.

## Ducted and floor console units

Ducted systems have a return air filter in a ceiling grille, an indoor unit in the roof space, and ducts to each room. We clean the filter and the indoor unit; heavily soiled ducts are a bigger job we'll discuss with you. Floor consoles clean much like wall units, with the advantage that they're at floor level.
