## Heat pumps for Aranui state houses and newer homes

In Aranui, the heat pump you need depends on how old the house is. If it's rented, the Healthy Homes heating standard comes into it too. Aranui is in east Christchurch. A big share of its homes are 1950s and 60s state houses. Lots are rentals, including public housing. Newer homes have also replaced some of the older ones in recent years.

- **1950s and 60s homes:** state houses and private homes alike. Most are single-storey on piles, clad in weatherboard or brick. Unless they've been upgraded, the insulation is light. Heat escapes through the floor and ceiling. So they need more capacity for their size. Insulate first, and a smaller unit can do the job. We cover the lounge layout below.
- **Newer homes and townhouses:** built to today's standards. They need far less heating for the same floor area. One high wall unit usually heats an open plan living area.
- **Rentals:** old or new, they must meet a minimum heating capacity in the main living room. Details below.

## Heat pumps for Aranui rentals

Letting a home in Aranui? The [Healthy Homes heating standard](https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/healthy-homes/heating-standard/) applies. You need one or more fixed heaters that can directly heat the main living room. They must be at least 1.5 kW. A heat pump must have a thermostat. An open fire won't meet it, and nor will an unflued combustion heater.

The capacity you need is worked out for each living room. Tenancy Services publishes a heating assessment tool alongside the standard. Once you've got the figure, we'll quote a heat pump that meets it. In an older lounge with no insulation, go a bit above the minimum. That keeps the room comfy on frosty mornings.

## Heat pump installation in older Aranui state houses

In an original state house, the lounge is usually its own room at the front. The kitchen sits behind it. One high wall unit, blowing down the length of the lounge, heats it well.

If you want the bedrooms warm too, they need their own unit. Or a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/). There's often space under the floor, so we can run the pipes under the house to the outdoor unit.

## Newer Aranui homes and townhouses

Newer homes in Aranui are better insulated. A smaller unit can heat an open plan living area.

In a townhouse with shared walls, the outdoor unit has to go where it won't bother next door. Christchurch measures heat pump noise at the boundary. [Boundary rules and consent](/heat-pump-boundary-rules-consent-nz/) explains how that works.

## Running costs in older Aranui houses

A draughty older house costs more to heat. The heat pump has to run longer to hold the same temperature. A few simple fixes help:

- **Curtains:** thick ones that reach the floor
- **Draughts:** stop the gaps round doors
- **Insulation:** in the ceiling and under the floor, if it's not there yet

With these in place, a unit that's sized right can cruise through the evening instead of working flat out.
