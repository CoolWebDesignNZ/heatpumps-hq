## The problem heat pump zoning solves

Without zoning, a ducted heat pump heats every room at once. It doesn't matter if they're empty. It has one thermostat, usually on the wall controller. And it sends air to every vent whenever it runs. Warm the lounge on a winter afternoon and the empty bedrooms get warmed too. Turn it on for the bedrooms at night and the living areas get heated as well.

Zoning splits the house into areas you can switch and control on their own. So the system heats the rooms you're actually in.

## How ducted heat pump zoning works

Ducted zoning has four parts.

- **Dampers.** These are motorised flaps in the ducts. They sit in the main duct to each zone, or in the duct to each room. They open to let air through and close to shut it off.
- **Zone controller.** A wall controller, and often a phone app. You use it to turn zones on and off and set times.
- **Temperature sensors.** Simpler systems just switch zones on and off. Better ones have a sensor in each zone. They adjust the damper or airflow to hold that zone at its own temperature.
- **Airflow management.** When several zones close, the extra air has to go somewhere. The system either slows the fan or lets air bypass. A good design stops the open zones getting noisy.

## Zone on and off, or temperature in each zone

There are two levels of zoning, and only one lets rooms sit at different temperatures.

1. **On and off zoning.** You choose which zones get air. The heat pump still works to one thermostat. So all the open zones aim for the same temperature.
2. **Temperature control in each zone.** Each zone has its own sensor and setting. The lounge can sit warmer than the bedrooms while both are on.

## Planning the zones

Set the zones by how you live in the house, not just the floor plan.

- **Living and bedrooms:** living areas and kitchen in one zone, bedrooms in another.
- **Home office:** its own zone if you use it during the day.
- **Two storeys:** upstairs and downstairs as separate zones.
- **Spare rooms:** a guest room or rumpus room that stays shut off most of the time.

The heat pump also needs enough places to push air when only one small zone is open. That limits how small a zone can be. It's one reason zoning works best when it's designed with the duct layout. Tacked on later, it's harder to get right.

## Heat pump zoning in Christchurch houses

The shape of the house and how you use it set the zones. In Christchurch, these are the layouts to think about.

- **Hill homes on more than one level.** In [Cashmere](/cashmere/) and [Westmorland](/westmorland/), houses follow the slope, with living areas and bedrooms on different floors. Warm air climbs the stairwell, so one setting for the whole house doesn't suit. We set each level up as its own zone.
- **Big homes lived in end to end.** One wall unit seldom heats a big [Fendalton](/fendalton/) home, so many end up ducted, multi-room or a mix. On a ducted system, zoning lets you heat the bedrooms at night and leave the living areas off.
- **A second lounge that sits empty.** Some [Russley](/russley/) homes have a formal lounge that's only used when guests come. Heating it every day wastes power, and leaving it cold lets damp settle in. A ducted zone you can close off and open when you need it handles both.
- **New houses on the edge of the city.** Building in [Marshland](/marshland/)? Talk to us before the linings go up. Zoning works best when it's planned with the duct layout.

## Adding zone control to an existing ducted heat pump

Got a ducted heat pump that heats the whole house? Zone control can often be added later, if the system passes these checks.

- **The indoor unit:** does it work with the zone controller you want?
- **The duct layout:** can each zone be shut off with a damper?
- **The ducts:** what shape are they in? Leaking or crushed ducts undo the benefit.
- **Placement:** where can the controller and any zone sensors go?

If the system is old or the ducts are in bad shape, we may suggest a new system with zoning built in. We'll give you both options, each with a fixed price.

## Multi zone heating with other heat pump types

Zoning is a ducted system term. Wall units and floor consoles already have their own control on each indoor unit. So a [multi room heat pump](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/) is sometimes the simpler answer. It suits you if you want separate control of a few rooms and don't need everything hidden in the ceiling.
