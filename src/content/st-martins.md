## Choosing heat pumps for St Martins homes

St Martins sits on the flat south of the city centre, with the Heathcote river winding along its edge and the Port Hills rising to the south. Almost all of the suburb is level ground. That shapes the work. There are no long pipe runs up a slope and no decks to bracket on. The job is about the house itself: how old it is, how well it holds heat, and where the outdoor unit can sit with clear air around it.

- **Older villas and bungalows.** Many St Martins streets are lined with homes from the early and mid 1900s. They often have timber floors, single glazing and ceilings that have been insulated but not always well. These homes need more heating capacity than their floor area suggests.
- **State and post-war houses.** Some streets have simpler, smaller houses from the 1950s to 1970s. They tend to have low ceilings and small living rooms, and one high wall unit usually heats the main room comfortably.
- **Rebuilt and newer homes.** A number of houses were rebuilt or renovated after the earthquakes, and newer townhouses have gone up on subdivided sections. These hold heat well, so a smaller unit often does the job.

We look at insulation, glazing and room layout at the quote. Getting the [sizing](/heat-pump-sizing/) right matters more here than the brand on the box.

## Heat pump installation in St Martins

Most St Martins installs are a single high wall unit in the living area, sometimes with a second unit in a hallway or bedroom. The outdoor unit usually goes on the ground on raised feet, down the side of the house or against a fence, where it gets airflow and stays out of the way.

A few things come up again and again on the flat:

- **Frosty mornings.** St Martins gets cold winter mornings, and the outdoor unit will frost up and run a defrost cycle. That's normal. A [cold climate model](/cold-climate-heat-pumps/) handles it better and keeps heating through the cold snap.
- **Nor'westers.** The dry wind that comes over the hills can be strong on exposed corners. We site the outdoor unit so it isn't taking the full force, and check the bracket or feet are solid.
- **Older wiring and switchboards.** Some of the older houses here have switchboards that need a check before a new circuit goes in. We flag that at the quote so there are no surprises.

If the house has a long open plan living and dining area, a [single room unit](/single-room-heat-pump-installation/) may not reach the far end. That's where [multi-room](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/) setups or a small [ducted system](/ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/) come in.

## Servicing heat pumps in St Martins

A yearly [service](/heat-pump-servicing/) is the cheapest way to keep a heat pump running well through winter. On the flat, dust and pollen settle on the outdoor coil and clog the indoor filter. A dirty unit works harder and costs more to run.

A service checks the filters and coil, the refrigerant charge, the drain, the outdoor unit and the electrical connections. If you want the unit checked before the cold sets in, a [seasonal tune-up](/heat-pump-seasonal-tune-ups/) in autumn is a good habit. Homes near the river can also run damp in winter, and a service is a good time to check the [dehumidifying](/heat-pump-dehumidification/) side is working.
