## Heat pumps for Prebbleton's new houses and older homes

Prebbleton sits between Christchurch and Lincoln, in Selwyn District. The heat pump you need here comes down to two things. When the house was built. And how its rooms join up.

- **New subdivision homes:** most are built to current insulation standards, often with double glazing. Many have one big kitchen, dining and family room. That room holds its heat. So one high wall unit, sized for the whole floor area, will warm it.

  Go too big and the unit hits temperature fast. Then it turns off and on all evening and uses more power. It won't do much for bedrooms at the far end. Want them warm at night? Plan a small second unit, or [multi-room heads](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/), from the start.
- **Older village homes:** the houses that came before the subdivisions usually have smaller rooms with doors between them. A lounge unit stays in the lounge. These homes lose more heat through the ceiling, the floor and old windows. We look in the roof space and under the floor before we settle on a size.
- **Lifestyle blocks and bigger sections:** there's room to put the outdoor unit well away from bedrooms and the boundary. A house with more than one living area usually suits more than one indoor unit, or a ducted system.

When we quote, we measure every room. We note the glazing and insulation. Then we work out what each unit has to put out on a frosty Selwyn morning.

## Plan heat pump installation during a Prebbleton build

Building in Prebbleton? Plan the heat pump before the linings go on. At that stage:

- **Pipes and cable:** can run inside the walls, not in ducting on the outside.
- **Condensate drains:** can be plumbed in properly, not run down the outside wall.
- **Ducting:** for a ducted system, it can go through the roof space with no ceiling patching.
- **Outdoor unit:** you can plan its spot around the bedrooms, the outdoor living area and the neighbours.

Talk to your builder and to us early. The install ends up simpler and tidier.

## Wall, multi-room or ducted heat pumps for Prebbleton homes

If the home's already built, you've usually got three options:

- **A single wall unit:** it can heat a well insulated open plan living area.
- **A [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/):** adds smaller units in bedrooms, all on one outdoor unit.
- **A ducted system:** heats every room through ceiling vents. The indoor unit hides in the roof space.

Choosing [ducted vs split](/ducted-vs-split-heat-pumps-christchurch/) comes down to your roof space, and which rooms you want warm. Whatever you pick, we size it from your rooms, windows and insulation.

## Larger Prebbleton sections and rural blocks

On a bigger section, the outdoor unit can usually sit well back from the boundary. Then noise is less of a worry. It still needs raised feet or a bracket, so defrost water drains away on frosty mornings. It needs good airflow round it too.

Whoever wires it in has to be a licensed electrical worker. They'll make sure the switchboard can take the new circuit.

In Prebbleton, heat pump noise comes under the Selwyn District Plan. Where the outdoor unit sits decides how much noise reaches next door. So we place it with the [boundary rules](/heat-pump-boundary-rules-consent-nz/) in mind.
