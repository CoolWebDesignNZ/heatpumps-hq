## Plan heat pump installation during a Prebbleton build

If you're building in Prebbleton, the best time to plan a heat pump is before the linings go on. At that stage:

- **Pipes and cable** can run inside the walls instead of in external ducting.
- **Condensate drains** can be plumbed in properly rather than run down the outside.
- **Ducting** for a ducted system can go through the roof space with no ceiling patching.
- **The outdoor unit's spot** can be planned around bedrooms, the outdoor living area and your neighbours.

Talk to your builder and to us early, and the install becomes simpler and tidier.

## Wall, multi-room or ducted heat pumps for Prebbleton homes

In a home that's already built, the choice usually comes down to three options. A single wall unit can heat a well insulated open plan living area. A [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/) adds smaller units in bedrooms, all running from one outdoor unit. A ducted system heats every room through ceiling vents, with the indoor unit hidden in the roof space. Our [ducted vs split guide](/ducted-vs-split-heat-pumps-christchurch/) sets out the differences. We size whichever you choose from the rooms, windows and insulation of your house.

## Larger Prebbleton sections and rural blocks

On a bigger section the outdoor unit can usually sit well away from the boundary, which makes noise less of a concern. It still needs raised feet or a bracket so defrost water drains away on frosty mornings, and good airflow around it. Whoever wires it in must be a licensed electrical worker, and they confirm the switchboard can take the new circuit.
