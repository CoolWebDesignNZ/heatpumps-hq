## What a good heat pump installation starts with

It starts with a look at your house, before we quote. Get the size or the spot wrong and the heat pump costs more to run. It never feels quite right. So we check each room's size and shape. We check the insulation in the ceiling, walls and floor. We look at how much glass there is and which way it faces. And we ask how you use the room on a winter day.

That visit tells us three things.

- How much heat the room really needs.
- Which type of indoor unit suits it.
- Where both units can go. We want a short pipe run, warm air reaching where you sit, and a quiet outdoor unit at the boundary.

## Choosing the right heat pump system

It depends on the rooms you want heated. These are the usual options.

- **One high wall unit** for an open plan living area. See [single room heat pumps](/single-room-heat-pump-installation/).
- **A floor console** where there's no good wall space up high, or under a window.
- **Two or more indoor units.** Each on its own outdoor unit, or several on one. See [multi room heat pumps](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/).
- **A ducted system.** A vent in each room and nothing on the walls. See [ducted heat pumps](/ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/).

We fit **Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, Fujitsu and Toshiba**. We can talk you through [how the brands compare](/heat-pump-brands-comparison/). On site, we'll tell you which models we'd shortlist for your rooms.

## What a heat pump install includes

Every install we do has the same six steps.

1. **Indoor unit.** Fitted level on a bracket. It's high enough to throw warm air across the room. It's clear of curtains and furniture.
2. **Pipes and cable.** The refrigerant pipes, control cable and drain go through one neat hole in the wall. Outside, they run in a cover that matches the house.
3. **Outdoor unit.** On raised feet, a pad or a wall bracket. It has space round it for air to move and defrost water to drain.
4. **Electrical connection.** Done by a licensed electrical worker. There's an isolating switch next to the outdoor unit.
5. **Commissioning.** We pressure test and vacuum the pipes, then let the refrigerant in. We run the system in heating and cooling to check it's working properly.
6. **Handover.** We show you the remote. We show you the settings that make the biggest difference to comfort and running cost. And we show you how to clean the filters.

## Where the outdoor unit goes

It needs free air on all sides. It needs a spot where frost melt can drain. And it needs to be away from your neighbour's bedroom windows. People think about the outdoor unit least. But where it goes decides whether the system stays quiet and copes with frost. Corners and tight alcoves make it louder. In Christchurch, the District Plan sets [limits on noise at the neighbouring property](/heat-pump-boundary-rules-consent-nz/).

## Replacing an older system

Got a heat pump that's failing or past its best? We check whether the new unit can use the old pipe route, drain and electrical circuit. Sometimes it can. Sometimes the new model needs a different pipe size or a new circuit. We'll tell you which before you agree to anything. And if the old unit is worth [repairing](/heat-pump-repair/), we'll say so.

## What changes the price of a heat pump installation

These things change the [price of a heat pump installation](/heat-pump-installation-cost-christchurch/):

- the unit's size and type
- the distance between the indoor and outdoor units
- how the walls and floor are built
- how easy the outdoor spot is to get to
- whether the switchboard needs work

No two installs cost the same. The quote is free. The price is fixed before we start.
