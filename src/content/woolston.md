## Choosing heat pumps for Woolston homes

Woolston sits on the flat, east of the city centre and close to the Heathcote River. Most of the suburb is level ground, so there are no hill sections to plan around. The housing is a mix, and the mix shapes the heat pump choice.

- **Older workers' cottages and villas.** Many of the streets closest to the old industrial area have small, older houses. They often have timber floors, single glazing and little insulation. They lose heat faster than their floor area suggests, so they need more capacity than a newer house of the same size.
- **Bungalows and state houses from the mid century.** These are common through the middle of Woolston. Insulation varies from house to house. We check the ceiling and underfloor at the quote, because better insulation can mean a smaller unit that costs less to run.
- **Newer townhouses and infill builds.** These hold heat well. One high wall unit usually heats an open plan living area. The harder part is outside, where the outdoor unit has to fit a narrow side yard with clear air around it.

## Heat pump installation in Woolston

A heat pump install in Woolston usually comes down to the house, not the suburb. On the flat, the outdoor unit can often sit at ground level on feet or a small pad, out of the way of the section. If the side yard is tight, a wall bracket keeps it off the ground and clear of the path.

We size every install to the room and the house. A small cottage with a closed-off living room needs a different unit from a draughty bungalow with an open plan kitchen and lounge. We look at the glazing, the insulation and how the rooms connect before we recommend a model. For a single room, a [single room heat pump](/single-room-heat-pump-installation/) is often the right start. If you want two or three rooms warm, a [multi room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/) can run off one outdoor unit.

Woolston's frosty winter mornings are worth planning for. Standard heat pumps lose some capacity as the outdoor temperature drops. A [cold climate heat pump](/cold-climate-heat-pumps/) is rated to keep its output on cold mornings, which suits the flat, inland side of the suburb.

## Servicing heat pumps in Woolston

A yearly [heat pump service](/heat-pump-servicing/) keeps a unit running efficiently. In an older Woolston house, the heat pump often works harder than in a well-insulated new build, so the filters clog sooner and the outdoor coil collects more dust and leaves. A service checks the filters, the coil, the drains and the refrigerant lines, and picks up small faults before they become breakdowns.

If you want the work on a set schedule, our [customised service plans](/customised-heat-pump-service-plans/) cover a yearly visit. For a unit that has sat unused through summer, a [seasonal tune up](/heat-pump-seasonal-tune-ups/) gets it ready for winter.

## Keeping a river-side Woolston house warm and dry

Parts of Woolston sit close to the Heathcote River, and older houses near the water can feel damp in winter. A heat pump that keeps the living areas at a steady temperature cuts condensation on the windows. On milder, muggy days, dry mode pulls moisture out of the air without heating the room much. See how [dry mode and dehumidification](/heat-pump-dehumidification/) work together. Ventilation and insulation still matter alongside the heat pump.
