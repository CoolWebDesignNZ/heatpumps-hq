## Fujitsu heat pump repair

A Fujitsu that needs repairing shows the same signs as any heat pump. It stops. A light on the indoor unit flashes. The heat fades, water drips, or a new noise starts. The job is to find the cause, not just treat the symptom.

We read any saved fault first. Then we test the parts it points to. That means the sensors, fans and control board. It also means the wiring between the units, and the gas circuit.

If a part has failed, we'll tell you which one. We'll say if it's in stock and quote the repair before we start. Sometimes it's something you can stop next time, like a blocked filter or a sagging drain hose. If so, we'll show you. [Fault finding](/heat-pump-troubleshooting/) lists everything we check.

## Fujitsu air conditioner repair

Lots of people call their Fujitsu an air conditioner. In summer, that's what it is. Cooling faults often turn up in the first hot week of the year. A drain that blocked over a winter of heating starts to drip. An outdoor unit choked with leaves can't get rid of heat.

If it cools but drips, it's usually the drain. If it runs but won't cool, it's more likely airflow or gas. We test first either way.

## Fujitsu heat pump service

A service keeps your Fujitsu putting out the heat it was sized for.

- **Indoors:** we wash the filters and clean the coil and fan
- **Drain:** we clear it and test it
- **Outdoors:** we clean the coil and clear space round the unit
- **Output:** we check it on heat and on cool

The full details are under [heat pump servicing](/maintenance-servicing/). Does it smell when it starts? Then the coil and fan need a deeper [clean](/heat-pump-cleaning/).

## Fujitsu heat pump service in Christchurch homes

Where your Fujitsu sits changes what it needs between services.

- **Salt on the east side.** In [Parklands](/parklands/) and [Queenspark](/queenspark/), easterly winds carry some salt in from the coast. Give the outdoor coil a gentle rinse now and then. We check it at every service.
- **Hot nor'west summers.** Out by the airport in [Harewood](/harewood/), a unit that runs hard on cooling all summer can clog its filters as fast as it does in winter. Rinse them every few weeks while it's working hard.
- **Homes that came with a heat pump.** A unit in a [Dallington](/dallington/) home may not have had a service in years. A clean of the filters, coil and fan, and a drain check, is the place to start before you pay for a repair.
- **Frosty mornings.** In [Somerfield](/somerfield/), the streets get their share of winter frosts. We check the outdoor unit sits up on feet or a bracket, so defrost water drains and can't freeze around the base.
