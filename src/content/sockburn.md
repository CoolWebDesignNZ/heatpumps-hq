## Heat pumps for older Sockburn houses and newer units

How warm a heat pump keeps your Sockburn home depends less on the unit itself. It depends more on how much heat the house loses, and how the rooms are divided up. Sockburn has weatherboard and brick houses from the middle of last century. It also has newer units and townhouses where sections have been split.

- **Older weatherboard and brick houses.** Many have a separate lounge, a kitchen at the back and bedrooms off a hall. The glazing is often original single glass, and the insulation is patchy. Heat escapes through the glass, the ceiling and the floor. Closed doors keep a lounge unit's warmth in the lounge.
- **Newer units and townhouses.** They're smaller and better sealed. One small high wall unit in the living area can heat the place. A two storey townhouse may need a second small unit upstairs.

Sockburn lies between Upper Riccarton and Hornby, on both sides of the main roads heading west. That affects upkeep and placement too. Homes near busy roads gather dust faster. Split sections leave little room outside.

## Heat pump installation in Sockburn starts with the living room

In an older Sockburn house, we start with the room you spend your evenings in. Size a wall unit for that room, close the doors, and it'll keep it warm. Try to heat the whole house from one unit and you end up oversized. The living room gets too hot while the bedrooms stay cold.

Need the bedrooms warm as well? A second unit or a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/) does it properly. At the quote we check the ceiling and underfloor insulation. We'll tell you if topping it up would let you go with a smaller unit.

## Heat pump service for Sockburn homes near busy roads

Some Sockburn homes back onto busy roads or commercial sites. Dust settles on the outdoor coil and indoor filters faster there. A dirty coil cuts heat output and makes the unit work harder. A yearly [service](/maintenance-servicing/) keeps the coil and filters clear. In between, rinse the filters every few weeks in winter.

## Sockburn back sections and shared driveways

Narrow Sockburn sections leave little room for the outdoor unit. Back sections on a shared driveway are the tightest. We find a spot with airflow, where it drains freely and won't get knocked. The Christchurch District Plan measures noise at the neighbouring site. So we also keep the unit away from next door's bedrooms, within the [boundary limits](/heat-pump-boundary-rules-consent-nz/).

## Repair or replace an older heat pump in Sockburn

Before you pay for a new heat pump, we find out what's actually failed in the old one. A unit that's run for many winters can lose output for a few reasons. The fan can wear. The drain can block. Refrigerant can leak out slowly. Plenty of those are worth a [heat pump repair](/heat-pump-repair/) instead of a new unit. If replacing it makes more sense, we'll tell you. Then we'll quote it.
