## Mixed older housing in Sockburn

Sockburn lies between Upper Riccarton and Hornby, either side of the main roads heading west. Its housing is a mix: weatherboard and brick houses from the middle of last century and newer units and townhouses where sections have been split.

Older houses here often have a separate lounge, a kitchen at the back and bedrooms off a hall, with original single glazing and patchy insulation. The newer units are smaller and better sealed. Both can be warm with a heat pump, but the plan for each is different.

## Heat pump installation in Sockburn starts with the living room

In an older Sockburn house, we start with the room you spend your evenings in. A wall unit sized for that room, with the doors closed, will keep it warm. Trying to heat the whole house from one unit leads to an oversized heat pump running in a room that's too hot while the bedrooms stay cold.

If the bedrooms need heat as well, a second unit or a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/) does it properly. We check the ceiling and underfloor insulation at the quote and tell you if topping it up would let you choose a smaller unit.

## Heat pump service for Sockburn homes near busy roads

Some Sockburn homes back onto busy roads or commercial sites, and dust settles on outdoor coils and indoor filters faster there. A dirty coil cuts heat output and makes the unit work harder. A yearly [service](/maintenance-servicing/) keeps the coil and filters clear. Between services, rinse the filters every few weeks in winter.

## Sockburn back sections and shared driveways

Narrow Sockburn sections, especially back sections on a shared driveway, leave little room for the outdoor unit. We pick a spot where the unit has airflow, drains freely and won't be knocked. The Christchurch District Plan measures noise at the neighbouring site, so we also keep it away from next-door bedrooms. The [boundary rules guide](/heat-pump-boundary-rules-consent-nz/) explains the limits.

## Repair or replace an older heat pump in Sockburn

Whether to repair or replace an older Sockburn heat pump depends on what has actually failed, so we find that out before you pay for a new one. A unit that has run for many winters can lose output from a worn fan, a blocked drain or a slow refrigerant leak, and many of those faults are worth fixing. If replacement is the better call, we'll say so and quote it at a fixed price. Our [heat pump repairs](/heat-pump-repair/) page explains the usual faults.
