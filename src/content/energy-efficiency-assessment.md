## What this assessment is

A full home energy audit covers everything that uses energy in a house: hot water, lighting, appliances, insulation and heating. That's not what we do.

We assess the **heating** side of your home. As heat pump specialists, we look at how each room is heated, why some rooms stay cold, whether your existing heat pumps are the right size and in good condition, and what would give you warmer rooms for less power. It's the same thinking we use for every install quote, done as a dedicated visit.

## What we look at

### The rooms

- **Size and layout.** Floor area, ceiling height, open stairwells and whether rooms open into each other.
- **Windows and glazing.** How much glass, which way it faces, single or double glazing, and the curtains.
- **Insulation we can see.** Whether there's ceiling insulation in the roof space and underfloor insulation where we can get to it. We don't test wall insulation or open up walls.
- **Draughts.** Gaps around doors, open fireplaces, unused chimneys and vents that let warm air out.

### How you use the house

Which rooms you need warm, at what times, and who's home during the day. A family home with someone home all day needs a different plan from a couple out from 8am to 6pm.

### Your existing heating

- **Heat pumps.** Age, brand, capacity against the room, filter and coil condition, outdoor unit placement and the settings you use.
- **Other heaters.** Wood burners, gas heaters, panel and fan heaters, and how they fit with the heat pumps.
- **Controls.** Timers, Wi-Fi controllers and whether they're set up to match your routine.

## What you get

We walk you through what we found on the day and explain what we'd change, in order of the difference each would make to comfort and power use. That might be:

- a service or repair to an existing heat pump
- changing settings or timers
- moving an outdoor unit that is boxed in
- a second heat pump for a room that's never warm
- a replacement where the existing unit is too small or too old
- insulation or draught stopping to do first, by others

Any heat pump work comes with a fixed price before it starts. Insulation and other building work we don't do, we'll tell you about so you can get it done by someone who does.

## What we don't do

So there's no confusion:

- We don't issue an energy rating, certificate or a certified assessment for a house.
- We don't do thermal imaging, blower door tests or wall insulation testing.
- We don't assess hot water, lighting or appliances.

## Rentals and the Healthy Homes heating standard

Landlords must provide one or more fixed heaters that can directly heat the main living room, of at least 1.5 kW and meeting the room's minimum required heating capacity. Open fires and unflued combustion heaters don't count, and a heat pump or electric heater must have a thermostat. Electric heaters other than heat pumps can't be used where the required capacity is over 2.4 kW, apart from topping up qualifying heating installed before 1 July 2019 ([Tenancy Services, heating standard](https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/healthy-homes/heating-standard/)).

The required capacity can be worked out with the government's [Heating Assessment Tool](https://tools.tenancy.govt.nz/heating-tool), the formula in the regulations, or a professional assessment. Once you have that figure, we can supply and install a heat pump that meets it.

## Why it's worth doing before buying

A heat pump bought without looking at the house is a guess. Two homes with the same floor area can need very different units because of glass, insulation and layout. An assessment means the heat pump you buy, from us or anyone, is the right one for the house. More on that in our [sizing guide](/heat-pump-sizing/).
