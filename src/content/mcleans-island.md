## Rural homes on the city's fringe

McLeans Island is mostly open, rural land towards the Waimakariri River, and the homes here sit on lifestyle blocks and farm properties rather than suburban sections. Houses tend to be detached, often large, and fully exposed to the weather crossing the plains.

That changes the job. On a rural block, noise at the boundary is rarely the deciding factor because neighbours are far away. Shelter, frost and the size of the house are what matter.

## Heating a bigger house

A larger rural home with living areas at one end and bedrooms at the other is hard to heat from a single wall unit. The choices we usually price are:

- **[Ducted heating](/ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/)**, with a vent in every room and the indoor unit in the roof space. Good for whole-house comfort and clear walls.
- **Two or three wall units** in the rooms used every day, each on its own outdoor unit or on a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/).

We size from each room, the insulation, the windows and how you use the house. On an exposed site, we size for frosty mornings rather than mild afternoons, because a heat pump's output falls as the outside air gets colder.

## Wind, frost and the outdoor unit

The outdoor unit on a rural property should sit out of the prevailing nor'west wind, on a raised base well clear of the ground. Frost and long grass can block airflow, and defrost water needs to drain away from paths. Stock and machinery are another reason to choose the spot carefully; a small fence or a wall bracket keeps the unit out of harm's way.

## Servicing a rural heat pump

Dust from paddocks and gravel drives gets into outdoor coils and indoor filters. A yearly [service](/maintenance-servicing/) before winter cleans both and checks the drain and refrigerant performance. If a unit stops heating, our [repairs](/heat-pump-repair/) cover all the major brands.

## Heat pump or log burner

Many rural homes have a log burner already. A heat pump doesn't need firewood carted, stacked or lit, it heats on a timer before you get up, and it can cool the house on hot nor'west days. A burner keeps working through a power cut. Some owners on McLeans Island keep both: the heat pump for everyday heating, the burner for the coldest nights. We'll size the heat pump on the basis that it carries the house on its own.
