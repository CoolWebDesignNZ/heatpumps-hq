## What to have ready for a heat pump quote

Have a few details handy when you call. Then we can give you a useful answer straight away.

**For a new heat pump**

- Which rooms you want heated, and roughly how big they are. Several rooms can run off one outdoor unit with a [multi-room installation](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/).
- Whether the ceiling and underfloor are insulated. Whether the windows are single or double glazed.
- Photos of where the outdoor unit could go. Show the wall, the ground below it and the gap to the fence.

**For a repair or service**

- The brand. And the model number on the side of the indoor unit, if you can see it.
- What's wrong. Not heating, leaking, noisy, smelly or won't turn on.
- Any error code on the remote or display, or the pattern of flashing lights. Write it down or take a photo.
- A photo of the outdoor unit and what's around it.
- When it was last serviced, if you know.

Some faults have simple checks you can do before you call. [Heat pump troubleshooting](/heat-pump-problems-troubleshooting-guide/) lists them.
