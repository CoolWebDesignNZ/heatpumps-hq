## The Daikin ducted heat pump range in New Zealand

Daikin's New Zealand ducted range has five series on its NZ site: Slim-Line FBA, FDMA Modern Residential, Premium Inverter FDYA, Inverter Ducted FDYAN and Bulkhead FDYBA. The notes below follow how Daikin describes each one; check [Daikin's ducted heat pump page](https://www.daikin.co.nz/pages/ducted-heat-pumps) for the current models and full specifications.

| Series | What Daikin designed it for |
| --- | --- |
| Slim-Line FBA | Installs where ceiling space is at a premium, including bedrooms and heating several rooms |
| FDMA Modern Residential | A single split ducted unit with a depth of 700mm, designed for New Zealand homes, using R32 refrigerant |
| Premium Inverter FDYA | Whole-home heating and cooling for small to large houses, including retrofits of old R22 ducted systems |
| Inverter Ducted FDYAN | Whole-home systems in houses with limited roof space and outdoor space |
| Bulkhead FDYBA | Heating or cooling one area, in drop ceilings and shallow ceilings |

Daikin notes that with a ducted system the only parts visible inside the house are the controller, the return air grille and the discharge grilles.

## Matching a Daikin series to your house

The right Daikin unit comes down to three things we check on site:

- **Ceiling space.** How much clear height and access there is in the roof. A shallow or crowded space points towards the slimmer series; a roomy one gives more choice.
- **What you're heating.** A whole house, the bedroom wing, or one room or area. A bulkhead unit for a single area is a very different job from a whole-home system.
- **What's there now.** If you have an old ducted system, its ducting, outlets and pipe routes may be reusable. We inspect them before we count on it.

From there we size the unit for the heat each room loses, not the total floor area alone, and lay out the outlets and return air grille.

## Zoning options for Daikin ducted systems

Daikin lists two ways to add room by room control on its ducted page:

- **Airzone VAF.** A variable airflow zoning system that Daikin says is compatible with its residential and commercial ducted indoor units. It gives individual temperature control in each zone and adjusts fan speed to suit.
- **AirHub Touch zone controller.** A touch screen zone controller for controlling the temperature of individual rooms, with timers to switch zones on and off through the day.

Zoning suits houses where the bedrooms and living areas are used at different times. How it works in general is on our [heat pump zoning](/heat-pump-zoning/) page.

## Installing a Daikin ducted system

The install follows the same steps as any [ducted heat pump](/ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/): indoor unit fitted in the roof space or underfloor, insulated ducts run to each outlet, a return air grille with an accessible filter, the outdoor unit placed for airflow and noise, the electrical connection by a licensed electrical worker, and a full test in heating and cooling before handover. You get a fixed price before any work starts.
