## The Daikin ducted heat pump range in New Zealand

Daikin's New Zealand ducted range has five series. They're Slim-Line FBA, FDMA Modern Residential, Premium Inverter FDYA, Inverter Ducted FDYAN and Bulkhead FDYBA. The notes below follow how Daikin describes each one. For current models and full specs, check [Daikin's ducted heat pumps](https://www.daikin.co.nz/pages/ducted-heat-pumps).

| Series | What Daikin designed it for |
| --- | --- |
| Slim-Line FBA | Installs where ceiling space is at a premium, including bedrooms and heating several rooms |
| FDMA Modern Residential | A single split ducted unit with a depth of 700mm, designed for New Zealand homes, using R32 refrigerant |
| Premium Inverter FDYA | Whole-home heating and cooling for small to large houses, including retrofits of old R22 ducted systems |
| Inverter Ducted FDYAN | Whole-home systems in houses with limited roof space and outdoor space |
| Bulkhead FDYBA | Heating or cooling one area, in drop ceilings and shallow ceilings |

Daikin notes that with ducted, you only see three things inside. The controller, the return air grille and the discharge grilles.

## Matching a Daikin series to your house

We check three things on site.

- **Ceiling space.** How much clear height and access is there in the roof? A shallow or crowded space points to the slimmer series. A roomy one gives you more choice.
- **What you're heating.** The whole house, the bedroom wing, or one room or area. A bulkhead unit for one area is a very different job from a whole-home system.
- **What's there now.** Got an old ducted system? Its ducts, vents and pipe routes might be reusable. We check them before we count on it.

Then we size the unit for the heat each room loses, not just the total floor area. And we lay out the vents and the return air grille.

## Zoning options for Daikin ducted systems

Daikin lists two ways to get room by room control.

- **Airzone VAF.** A variable airflow zoning system. Daikin says it works with its home and commercial ducted indoor units. Each zone gets its own temperature control. The fan speed adjusts to suit.
- **AirHub Touch zone controller.** A touch screen controller for setting the temperature in each room. It has timers to switch zones on and off through the day.

Zoning suits houses where the bedrooms and living areas get used at different times. It works the same way with any brand. See [heat pump zoning](/heat-pump-zoning/).

## Installing a Daikin ducted system

It's the same steps as any [ducted heat pump](/ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/).

- The indoor unit goes in the roof space or under the floor.
- Insulated ducts run to each vent.
- A return air grille goes in, with a filter you can reach.
- The outdoor unit goes where it gets good airflow and stays quiet.
- A licensed electrical worker does the connection.
- We test it fully in heating and cooling before handover.

We agree a set price with you before the job starts.
