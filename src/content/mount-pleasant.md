## Heat pumps for Mount Pleasant homes with big north windows

On Mount Pleasant, it's the glass that sets the size of the heat pump, more than the floor area. Many houses here climb the Port Hills facing north, over the estuary and the city. They have big windows to take in the view. On a clear winter day, the sun can warm a north-facing living area by lunchtime. Once the sun drops, that same glass gives the heat away fast. So we size for the evening and early morning, when the house is coldest. We don't size for the sunny afternoon.

- **Rebuilt and heavily repaired homes.** There are plenty here since the earthquakes. They're usually well insulated, so the windows are where most heat goes. A high wall unit on an inside wall can heat an open plan living area on its own, if it has the capacity to cover the glass.
- **Older houses that haven't been upgraded.** They lose heat through the ceiling, the floor and single glazing, not just the view windows. Their rooms tend to be closed off from each other. A lounge unit won't warm the bedrooms. These homes often suit a small unit in each room you use. Insulate the ceiling and underfloor first, and each of those units can be smaller.

At the quote we measure each room. We note how much glass faces north and check the insulation. Then we settle on a size.

## Heat pumps that cool Mount Pleasant's sunny rooms

The glass that soaks up winter sun can make a room far too hot in summer. A heat pump cools as well as heats, so it pays its way in both seasons. In a room like that, the cooling load can be higher than the heating load. We check both before we choose the unit. Upstairs bedrooms facing north often do well with a small unit of their own. With a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/), those bedroom heads can share the living room's outdoor unit.

## Heat pump installation on steep Mount Pleasant sections

Flat ground is scarce on Mount Pleasant. The outdoor unit usually ends up on a wall bracket, a deck or a small platform. Four things decide the spot:

- **Weight.** The wall or deck framing has to hold the unit securely.
- **Noise and vibration.** We use anti-vibration mounts. We keep the unit away from your bedroom windows and your neighbour's.
- **Drainage.** Defrost water has to run off cleanly. It can't drip onto steps or a path.
- **Access.** Some sites are up long flights of steps. We plan for that at the quote visit.

Mount Pleasant is in Christchurch City. The District Plan's [boundary noise limits](/heat-pump-boundary-rules-consent-nz/) apply to every outdoor unit.
