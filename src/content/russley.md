## Heat pump installation in Russley's brick homes

Most Russley homes are brick and tile family houses from the 1960s to the 1980s with separate living rooms and a bedroom wing, and that layout decides the heat pump. Heat from one unit in the family room won't reach a bedroom wing down a hall, so one heat pump rarely heats the whole house well. Many owners started with a single unit in the family room and now want warmth in the bedrooms too.

- **Adding to an existing family room unit:** a small, quiet wall unit in each bedroom you use, or a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/) that runs the bedrooms from one new outdoor unit.
- **Heating the whole house:** [ducted heating](/ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/) where the ceiling space allows, with a vent in each room and nothing on the walls.
- **A formal lounge in some homes** that's used now and then gets its own small unit or a zone you can close off, covered below.
- **Larger homes on bigger sections** have more rooms to cover and longer pipe runs, so they more often go ducted or multi-room.

Homes of this age can have thin or settled ceiling insulation, and the single glazed windows lose heat on frosty nights. At the quote we check both, because they decide how big each unit needs to be. The suburb sits beside Russley Road on the western edge of Christchurch, close to the airport and the golf course, with many homes on quiet cul-de-sacs.

## Choosing indoor units for a Russley home

Not every room suits a high wall unit. In a lounge with a picture window and little wall above it, a [floor console](/single-room-heat-pump-installation/) can sit under the sill. In a bedroom, a small, quiet wall unit is usually best. In a house with good ceiling space, [ducted heating](/ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/) can serve every room with nothing on the walls.

We size each indoor unit from the room it heats, the insulation above it and the glass in it, then match the outdoor unit or units to that.

## Heat pumps exposed to Russley's western weather

Russley's position on the plains means hard frosts on clear nights and nor'west gales in spring. A heat pump has to defrost its outdoor coil on icy mornings, and it does that better when the unit sits clear of the ground with water draining freely away. On the wind side, a unit can lose airflow. We look at shelter as part of choosing the spot. If your house is especially exposed, our [cold climate heat pumps](/cold-climate-heat-pumps/) guide explains which features help.

## Heating a Russley formal lounge you rarely use

Some Russley homes have a second, formal lounge that sits empty most evenings. Heating it every day wastes power, but leaving it cold lets damp settle into the room. A small unit that can be switched on when guests come, or a zone on a ducted system that can be closed off, keeps the option open without paying to heat an empty room.

## Neighbours and outdoor unit noise in Russley

Russley is under the Christchurch District Plan, which judges outdoor unit noise at the neighbouring site rather than setting a distance from the fence. Our [boundary rules guide](/heat-pump-boundary-rules-consent-nz/) sets out the night limit and how to place a unit to stay under it.
