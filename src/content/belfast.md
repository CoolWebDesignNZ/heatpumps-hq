## Heat pumps for Belfast homes, new and established

Belfast is at the northern edge of Christchurch. The heat pump you need here depends on where your home is. Is it in one of the big new subdivisions, or the older part of the settlement?

- **New subdivision homes:** well insulated and double glazed. The job is getting the size right, not adding more power. Most of our Belfast work is in these houses. More on them below.
- **Older homes:** these have the separate rooms you'd expect in an established settlement. A lounge, a kitchen, and bedrooms off a hall. They lose more heat through the ceiling and windows. One lounge unit won't heat the bedrooms.
- **Homes facing open land:** out toward the Waimakariri River, houses get more wind and frost than those deeper in the suburb. We put the outdoor unit on a sheltered wall, up on feet or a bracket. That way defrost water drains, and the wind isn't fighting the fan.

## Heat pump installation in a new Belfast build

A new Belfast build is well insulated and double glazed. It holds heat. That makes it cheaper to heat. It also means a unit that's too big will overshoot and keep cutting in and out. We size every install from your rooms, windows and insulation, and how you use the house.

New homes in Belfast tend to follow a pattern. An open plan kitchen, dining and living area. A separate lounge or media room. Three or four bedrooms. There are two good ways to heat that:

- **Wall units:** a large one in the living area, plus smaller ones in the bedrooms. Or a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/) sharing one outdoor unit.
- **A [ducted system](/ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/):** vents in each room, the indoor unit in the roof space, and one control for the whole house.

## Plan Belfast heating before the linings go in

House not finished yet? Now's the time to plan the heating. Ducting needs a clear path through the ceiling space. Pipes for wall units look tidiest inside the walls, not in covers outside. Pick the outdoor unit's spot early. Then it ends up somewhere quiet and sheltered, and easy to reach for servicing.

## Adding heat pumps to older Belfast houses

In an older Belfast home, start with a wall unit sized just for the lounge. Check the ceiling insulation before you pay for more capacity. Topping it up can let a smaller unit do the job.

Lots of Belfast houses were built with one living area unit as standard. Adding bedroom units later is easy enough. Each bedroom needs an outside wall for the indoor unit, and a clear path for the pipes to the outdoor unit.
