- **High wall units**: the usual single-room heat pump

## Mitsubishi heat pump repairs

A Mitsubishi with a problem will often stop and flash the operation light on the indoor unit. On a wired controller, it shows a code instead. Other faults look different. The heat might be weak. Water might run down the wall. The outdoor unit might rattle, or there's a smell when it starts.

The code is only a starting point. It tells us which part or circuit picked up the problem. Our tests then find the real cause. From the couch, a bad sensor, a fan motor, a control board and a gas leak can all look much the same. [Fault finding](/heat-pump-troubleshooting/) is how we tell them apart.

Once we know what's failed, you get a fixed price. Sometimes the unit is too old for a repair to be worth it. If so, we'll say so, and price both the repair and a replacement.

## Mitsubishi heat pump service

Our Mitsubishi service cleans the unit inside and out and checks what it's putting out. Christchurch heat pumps work hard all winter. A dirty one gives you less heat for the same power. Here's what we do:

1. Take the filters out and wash them. Clean the indoor coil and fan.
2. Clear the drain tray and hose, then test them with water.
3. Check the outdoor unit. Clean its coil and pull away leaves and garden growth.
4. Run it on heat and on cool, to see it's putting out the heat it should.

Try to book your [heat pump servicing](/maintenance-servicing/) before winter. If the unit smells musty, it may need [heat pump cleaning](/heat-pump-cleaning/) too.

## Wi-Fi Control on Mitsubishi heat pumps

Mitsubishi Electric calls its own Wi-Fi system [Wi-Fi Control](https://www.mitsubishi-electric.co.nz/wifi/). It's a small Wi-Fi interface plus an app on your phone. You can buy it with a new heat pump. You can also add it to a compatible older model.

App stopped connecting? It's often the router, not the heat pump. Look for a new router, a changed password, or the 2.4 GHz band turned off. Every other brand has its own app, and we compare them under [smart heat pump systems](/smart-heat-pump-systems/).
