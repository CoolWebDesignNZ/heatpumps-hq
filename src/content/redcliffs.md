## Choosing heat pumps for Redcliffs homes, flat and hill

Two things decide the heat pump for a Redcliffs home. One is how well the house holds heat. The other is how much salt air off the estuary and the sea reaches the outdoor unit. Redcliffs runs along the coast road between Moncks Bay and Sumner. The estuary is on one side, with steep cliffs and hillside on the other. So nearly every house gets both wind and salt.

- **Bungalows on the flat streets.** Many have the living room at the front and bedrooms down a long hall. A high wall unit in the living area heats that end. Its warm air stops at closed bedroom doors, though. So a smaller second unit in the bedroom wing is the usual addition. If the ceiling, floor or windows haven't been upgraded, we check those first. They change the size of both units.
- **Family homes and newer infill on the flat.** These are better insulated and often open plan. One unit, sized right, can heat the living, dining and kitchen space. Want the bedrooms warm from the same outdoor unit? Go with a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/).
- **Houses stepping up the hillside.** Many are rebuilds now. So are some homes in the streets under the cliffs, which changed a lot after the earthquakes. A rebuild holds heat well. The job is less about capacity and more about mounting the outdoor unit and running the pipes.

Whatever the house, the outdoor unit has to be placed and cared for with salt air in mind.

## Heat pump installation near salt water in Redcliffs

The wind carries salt onto the outdoor unit's coil fins and metal casing. Over time, it can corrode them. That cuts how well the unit moves heat. So we aim to:

- **Shelter the unit.** The side of the house facing away from the water is usually best, as long as air can still move freely around it.
- **Keep it off the ground.** Raised feet or a bracket keep it clear of puddles and blown debris.
- **Leave room to clean it.** A unit jammed into a corner can't be rinsed or serviced properly.

On a coastal site, we follow each maker's install guidance, clearances included.

## Flat and hill sections in Redcliffs

On the hill, the outdoor unit usually goes on a wall bracket or a deck. That can mean a longer pipe run to the indoor unit. The drain line also needs a clear path downhill. On the flat, the limit is more often a narrow side yard, with the neighbour close by. Either way, we work out [heat pump sizing](/heat-pump-sizing/) from your rooms, insulation and windows.

## Looking after a coastal heat pump in Redcliffs

A unit near the water needs more care than one inland. Now and then, rinse the outdoor coil gently with a garden hose. Never use a water blaster. Clean the indoor filters every few weeks in winter. A yearly [professional clean](/heat-pump-cleaning/) covers the parts you can't reach. It also lets us spot corrosion early.
