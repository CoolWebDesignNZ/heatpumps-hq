## Who we are

Heat Pumps HQ is a heat pump installation, servicing and repair business in Christchurch. We follow the [Privacy Act 2020](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2020/0031/latest/LMS23223.html) and its [information privacy principles](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/). Nate Thompson owns the business and looks after privacy questions. You can reach him at [info@heatpumps-christchurch.co.nz](mailto:info@heatpumps-christchurch.co.nz).

## What we collect

This website only collects the personal information you type into the quote form:

- your name
- your phone number
- your suburb
- the type of job (installation, repair or service)
- any message you add

If you phone or email us instead, we keep what you tell us in the same way. If you email, we keep your email address too.

## What we don't collect

Apart from the quote form, the site collects nothing about you.

- **No analytics.** We don't run Google Analytics or any other visitor tracking on this site.
- **No tracking or advertising cookies.** The site doesn't set cookies to follow you round the web.
- **No mailing lists.** We don't add you to a newsletter or send you marketing.

## Why we collect it

We use your details for these things only:

- to contact you about your enquiry
- to prepare a quote
- to book and do the work
- to keep a record of the job, in case you need us again for servicing or a repair

We don't use them for anything else.

## How it's handled

When you send the quote form, your details go to our business email inbox. Only the people who need them to answer your enquiry and do the job see them. We don't sell or rent personal information. We don't share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it or you ask us to.

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe. We keep it only as long as we need it for the reasons above.

## Links to other sites

This site links to our Facebook page and to government websites. Once you leave our site, that site's own privacy policy applies.

## Your right to see and correct your information

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you. If it's wrong, you can ask us to correct it. Email [info@heatpumps-christchurch.co.nz](mailto:info@heatpumps-christchurch.co.nz) or call 03 242 1023. The Privacy Commissioner explains these rights on its [your privacy rights](https://www.privacy.org.nz/your-rights/your-privacy-rights/) page. That includes the rule that a business must respond to a request within 20 working days.

## Complaints

Think we've mishandled your information? Please tell us first, so we can put it right. If we can't sort it out, you can [make a complaint to the Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz/your-rights/making-a-complaint-to-the-privacy-commissioner/).

## Changes to this policy

If what the website collects changes, we'll update this page.

Send any questions about this policy to [info@heatpumps-christchurch.co.nz](mailto:info@heatpumps-christchurch.co.nz).
