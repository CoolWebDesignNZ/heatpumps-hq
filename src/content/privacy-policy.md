## Who we are

Heat Pumps HQ is a heat pump installation, servicing and repair business in Christchurch. We follow the [Privacy Act 2020](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2020/0031/latest/LMS23223.html) and its [information privacy principles](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/). Our owner looks after privacy questions for the business, and you can reach him at [info@heatpumps-christchurch.co.nz](mailto:info@heatpumps-christchurch.co.nz).

## What we collect

The only personal information this website collects is what you type into the quote form:

- your name
- your phone number
- your suburb
- the type of job (installation, repair or service)
- any message you add

If you phone or email us instead, we keep what you tell us in the same way, along with your email address if you write to us.

## What we don't collect

- **No analytics.** We don't run Google Analytics or any other visitor tracking on this site.
- **No tracking or advertising cookies.** The site doesn't set cookies to follow you around the web.
- **No mailing lists.** We don't add you to a newsletter or send marketing messages.

## Why we collect it

We use your details to contact you about your enquiry, prepare a quote, book and carry out the work, and keep a record of the job in case you need us again for servicing or a repair. We don't use them for anything else.

## How it's handled

When you send the quote form, your details are delivered to our business email inbox. They are seen only by the people who need them to answer your enquiry and do the job. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it with anyone else unless the law requires it or you ask us to.

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure, and we keep it only as long as we need it for the purposes above.

## Links to other sites

This site links to our Facebook page and to government websites. Once you leave our site, the other site's own privacy policy applies.

## Your right to see and correct your information

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it's wrong. Email [info@heatpumps-christchurch.co.nz](mailto:info@heatpumps-christchurch.co.nz) or call 03 242 1023. The Privacy Commissioner's page on [your privacy rights](https://www.privacy.org.nz/your-rights/your-privacy-rights/) explains these rights, including that a business must respond to a request within 20 working days.

## Complaints

If you think we've mishandled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. If we can't resolve it, you can [make a complaint to the Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz/your-rights/making-a-complaint-to-the-privacy-commissioner/).

## Changes to this policy

If what the website collects changes, we'll update this page.

Questions about this policy can go to [info@heatpumps-christchurch.co.nz](mailto:info@heatpumps-christchurch.co.nz).
