## Heat pumps for Heathcote Valley homes, flat and sloping

Two things decide the heat pump for a Heathcote Valley home. How much sun does your house get in winter? And how well does it hold heat? The valley sits at the foot of the Port Hills, at the Christchurch end of the road tunnel to Lyttelton. Through winter, the hills shade one side of it.

- **Hillside homes rebuilt or heavily repaired after the earthquakes.** These hold heat far better than the houses they replaced. One unit in the living area can often do the job. Add a small second unit if the bedrooms are on another level.
- **Older houses, mainly on the shady side.** They lose more heat and get little sun to make up for it. The living area needs more capacity. The bedrooms usually need their own heat, or a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/).
- **Houses on flat sections along the valley floor.** The outdoor unit has an easy spot beside the house, on a pad or raised feet.
- **Homes climbing the slopes on either side.** Think steep driveways, retaining walls and decks. Here the outdoor unit more often goes on a wall bracket or a deck. We check the structure can take it. We also make sure vibration won't travel through the floor into a bedroom.

At the quote we work out the heat load room by room. Then we plan the outdoor spot and the pipe route for your section.

## Heat pumps for the shady side of Heathcote Valley

The hills block the low winter sun. Through June and July, one side of the valley gets far less direct light than the other. A house on the shady side stays cooler all day. It leans harder on its heating. So we look at the insulation, the glazing and which way the living areas face. Floor area alone doesn't tell you enough. On the sunny side, a heat pump may only need to cover the evening and early morning.

## Heat pump installation on a sloping Heathcote Valley section

On a hillside, the tricky bit is often the route between the two units. The refrigerant pipes, the cable and the drain line all have to get from the indoor unit to the outdoor one. On a split-level house, that can mean going under a floor, through a garage or along a retaining wall. The drain line also needs a steady fall to a drain or the garden. We plan the route at the quote visit. That way the quote covers the whole job.
