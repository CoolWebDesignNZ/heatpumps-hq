## Choosing heat pumps for Huntsbury homes

Huntsbury sits on the lower slopes of the Port Hills, above the Heathcote Valley and beside Cashmere. It looks out over the city and across to the plains, and that height is what shapes the job. Homes here are mostly detached, on sections that step up or down the slope. Many were built in the middle of last century, with later rebuilds and additions mixed in. Some are newer, well insulated and tight. Others are older and leak warmth through timber floors, single glazing and ceilings.

- **Older hillside houses.** These often have living areas facing the view, which means big windows and a lot of glass to lose heat through. They need more capacity than the floor area alone suggests.
- **Rebuilt and newer homes.** They hold heat well. One high wall unit can usually carry an open plan living area. The harder question is where the outdoor unit sits.
- **Split-level and stepped homes.** Heat rises and settles in the upper rooms. A single unit in the hallway rarely reaches both ends. Zoning and a second unit often suit these layouts.

## Heat pump installation on the Huntsbury slopes

On a hill section, the outdoor unit is the part that needs thought. Huntsbury is exposed to the cold southerly and to the wind that funnels over the tops. An outdoor unit in the full face of that wind works harder and frosts up more often on clear winter nights. We look for a spot that is sheltered but still gets good airflow, often down the side of the house or under a deck. On steep ground the unit goes on a wall bracket or a stand, clear of the ground and out of the way of garden and runoff.

Pipe runs on a stepped section can be longer than they look. We plan the route at the quote visit, so the extra work and the mounting are in the quote rather than added later.

## Cold mornings and frost in Huntsbury

Height brings colder nights. Huntsbury gets frost on clear winter mornings more reliably than the flat, and the outdoor unit has to defrost itself through the cold months. A model rated for cold climate running copes with that better and holds its output when the temperature drops. Where a house is exposed and hard to keep warm, that is worth having.

We check the insulation at the quote. Ceiling and underfloor insulation, and draughts around older joinery, change how much heat the house needs. Insulating first can mean a smaller unit that costs less to run.

## Servicing and repair in Huntsbury

A heat pump on the hill deals with wind-blown dust, leaves and grit, and the outdoor coil collects it. A yearly [service](/heat-pump-servicing/) clears the coil and filters, checks the drain and tests the refrigerant charge, so the unit keeps its output through winter. If a unit stops heating, ices up and stays iced, or throws an error code, we take [repair jobs](/heat-pump-repair/) across the major brands and tell you what we find before any work goes ahead.
