## Daikin systems we work on

Daikin is one of the four brands we fit. We repair and service these Daikin types:

- **Wall-mounted split systems.** The single-room units in most Christchurch living areas and bedrooms.
- **Floor consoles.** Often fitted under a window, or where there's no good high wall.
- **Multi-split systems.** One outdoor unit runs two or more indoor units.
- **Ducted systems.** One unit in the ceiling or under the floor feeds a vent in each room. We fit these as [Daikin ducted heat pumps](/daikin-ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/).

## Daikin heat pump repair

A Daikin with a fault usually tells you in one of three ways. It stops and shows a code on the controller. It flashes a light on the indoor unit. Or it keeps running but heats poorly, leaks or gets noisy.

We test the unit to find the cause. We don't rely on the code alone. A code tells us where the unit noticed something wrong. That isn't always where the problem started. A low refrigerant warning, for example, is the sign of a leak somewhere else. [Fault finding](/heat-pump-troubleshooting/) is how we trace a code back to the real cause.

Before you call, note down three things. The model number from the sticker on the indoor unit. Any code on the controller. And what the unit was doing when the fault showed up. Once we know what's needed, you'll get a fixed price for the repair.

## Daikin air conditioning repairs in summer

Summer repairs are for cooling faults, and those have their own causes. Daikin New Zealand sells its split systems as air conditioning. In a Canterbury summer, that's exactly how they get used. There are three usual cooling faults:

- **A hot, blocked outdoor unit.** It sits in full sun with clogged fins.
- **A blocked drain.** It blocked up over winter, and starts dripping as soon as cooling starts.
- **A low refrigerant charge.** It was enough for heating, but shows up as weak cooling on a hot nor'west afternoon.

A dripping unit or a refrigerant leak needs [leak detection and repair](/leak-detection-repair/) before the cooling will come right.

## Daikin heat pump service

A yearly service keeps a Daikin heating at full output. It also catches small faults early. Ours covers:

- **Filters** washed, with the indoor coil and fan cleaned.
- **The drain tray and hose** cleared and tested.
- **The outdoor coil** cleaned, and the area around it cleared.
- **A performance check** with the unit running in heating and cooling.

Does the indoor unit smell musty when it starts? It needs a deeper clean of the coil and fan. That's [heat pump cleaning](/heat-pump-cleaning/), which goes further than routine [servicing](/maintenance-servicing/).

## Daikin controllers and Wi-Fi

Most Daikin wall units use a handheld remote. Many ducted and console systems use a wired wall controller. Daikin New Zealand also lists Wi-Fi controllers [on its website](https://www.daikin.co.nz/pages/controllers). Those are the Daikin Mobile Controller and Daikin Airbase. It lists zone controllers for ducted homes too. Has your controller stopped responding? We check the controller, its wiring and the indoor unit's board. Any of the three can be at fault. To run the heat pump from your phone, you need one of those Wi-Fi controllers. [Smart heat pump systems](/smart-heat-pump-systems/) explains the options.

## Repair or replace a Daikin?

On a Daikin in good condition, lots of faults are worth fixing. But say the compressor has failed on an older unit, or the parts aren't made any more. Then a new unit can be the better spend. We'll give you both options, with prices. We fit Daikin as well as Mitsubishi Electric, Fujitsu and Toshiba. So you can compare a like-for-like Daikin replacement with the others.
