## Daikin systems we work on

Daikin is one of the four brands we install, and we repair and service these Daikin types:

- **Wall-mounted split systems**, the single-room units in most Christchurch living areas and bedrooms
- **Floor consoles**, often fitted under a window or where there's no good high wall
- **Multi-split systems**, with one outdoor unit running two or more indoor units
- **Ducted systems**, with a single unit in the ceiling or under the floor feeding vents in each room. See our [Daikin ducted heat pumps](/daikin-ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/) page.

## Daikin heat pump repair

A Daikin that has developed a fault will usually tell you in one of three ways: it stops and shows a code on the controller, it flashes a light on the indoor unit, or it keeps running but heats poorly, leaks or gets noisy.

We test the unit to find the cause rather than relying on the code alone. A code tells us where the unit noticed something wrong, which isn't always where the problem started. A low refrigerant warning, for example, is the symptom of a leak somewhere else. The steps we follow are set out on our [fault finding](/heat-pump-troubleshooting/) page.

Before you call, note the model number from the sticker on the indoor unit, any code on the controller, and what the unit was doing when the fault appeared. You'll get a fixed price for the repair once we know what's needed.

## Daikin air conditioning repairs in summer

Daikin New Zealand sells its split systems as air conditioning, and in a Canterbury summer that's exactly what they're used for. Cooling faults have their own set of causes: an outdoor unit in full sun with blocked fins, a drain that has blocked over winter and starts dripping as soon as cooling starts, or a refrigerant charge that was enough for heating but shows up as weak cooling on a hot nor'west afternoon. Our [leak detection and repair](/leak-detection-repair/) page covers dripping units and refrigerant leaks.

## Daikin heat pump service

A yearly service keeps a Daikin heating at full output and catches small faults early. Ours covers:

- filters washed and the indoor coil and fan cleaned
- the drain tray and hose cleared and tested
- the outdoor coil cleaned and the area around it cleared
- a performance check with the unit running in heating and cooling

If the indoor unit smells musty when it starts, it needs a deeper clean of the coil and fan. See [heat pump cleaning](/heat-pump-cleaning/). Full service details are on our [servicing](/maintenance-servicing/) page.

## Daikin controllers and Wi-Fi

Daikin uses a handheld remote on most wall units and a wired wall controller on many ducted and console systems. Daikin New Zealand also lists Wi-Fi controllers, the Daikin Mobile Controller and Daikin Airbase, plus zone controllers for ducted homes, on its [controllers page](https://www.daikin.co.nz/pages/controllers). If your controller has stopped responding, we check the controller, its wiring and the indoor unit's board, since any of the three can be at fault. More on app control is on our [smart heat pump](/smart-heat-pump-systems/) page.

## Repair or replace a Daikin?

On a Daikin in good condition, many faults are worth repairing. When a compressor has failed on an older unit, or the parts are no longer available, a new unit can be the better spend. We'll give you both options with prices. Because we install Daikin as well as Mitsubishi Electric, Fujitsu and Toshiba, you can compare a like-for-like Daikin replacement with the others.
