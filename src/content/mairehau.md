## Heat pump installation for Mairehau homes

Most Mairehau homes are compact. That makes them easy to heat with a heat pump. The rooms are small and the walls are close together. The outdoor unit sits just a short way from the indoor one. So the pipe run is short and the job's simple. The age of the house decides the setup.

- **Modest mid-century weatherboard and brick houses.** These make up most of the suburb, former state houses included. One well-placed wall unit in the lounge heats the main living space. Bedrooms with the doors shut need their own heat. A cold floor usually means no underfloor insulation. So we'd fit a different size.
- **Newer houses toward the northern edge.** These are closer to the open land of Marshland. They're better insulated and often open plan. One unit can heat a bigger living area. Or a ducted system can heat every bedroom.

Mairehau is flat, in the north-east of Christchurch, between St Albans, Shirley and Marshland. Sections are a fair size. So there's usually more than one wall that can take the outdoor unit. We pick the one closest to the indoor unit that keeps noise away from the neighbours.

## Getting heat pump placement right in Mairehau

In a small older house, the best spot is often a lounge wall facing down the length of the room. Mount it high enough for the air to clear the furniture. Above a door into the hallway can work well. Some warmth then drifts toward the bedrooms. A [single room install](/single-room-heat-pump-installation/) like this is usually step one. You can add bedroom units later if you need them.

Did a previous owner put the heat pump somewhere awkward? Say a corner where it blows straight onto the couch? We can move it. Or we can swap it for a unit that's the right size.

## Insulation first in older Mairehau homes

Lots of older Mairehau homes have had some insulation added over the years. Not always under the floor, though. If your floor's cold to stand on, underfloor insulation may do as much for comfort as the heat pump. It also lets a smaller unit keep up. We note what we see at the quote visit, so you can decide.

## Newer Mairehau homes towards Marshland

The newer houses on Mairehau's northern side are better insulated. They're often open plan too. Here, one unit can heat a big living area. Some owners go for a ducted system, so every bedroom's heated without wall units. Ducted needs enough roof space for the indoor unit and the ducts. We check that before we price it against wall units.
