## Homes in Mairehau

Mairehau sits on flat ground in the north-east of Christchurch, between St Albans, Shirley and the more open land of Marshland. Much of its housing is modest mid-century stock: compact weatherboard and brick homes, including former state houses, on reasonable sections. Towards its northern edge the houses get newer.

A compact home is a good fit for a heat pump. The rooms are small, the walls are close together and the distance from the outdoor unit to the indoor unit is short, which keeps the install simple.

## Getting the placement right

In a small older house, the best spot is often a wall in the lounge that faces down the length of the room, high enough that the airflow clears the furniture. Above a door into the hallway can work well, because some warmth then drifts towards the bedrooms. A [single room install](/single-room-heat-pump-installation/) like this is usually the first step, with bedroom units added later if you need them.

If a previous owner put the heat pump in an awkward place, such as a corner that blows straight onto the couch, we can relocate it or replace it with a correctly sized unit.

## Insulation first

Many older Mairehau homes have had some insulation added over the years, but not always under the floor. If your floor is cold to stand on, underfloor insulation may do as much for comfort as the heat pump itself, and it lets a smaller unit keep up. We note what we see on the quote visit so you can decide.

## Newer homes towards Marshland

The newer houses on Mairehau's northern side are better insulated and often open plan. One unit can carry a large living area here, and some owners choose a ducted system so every bedroom is heated without wall units. We'll compare the two for your layout.
