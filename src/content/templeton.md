## Heat pumps for Templeton homes on the open plains

Templeton sits past Hornby on the road south, with paddocks and semi-rural sections all round. It gets cold, frosty mornings and nor'west wind. A heat pump here has to be sized for the coldest mornings, not an average day. The outdoor unit needs a spot out of the wind. It also needs somewhere for the defrost water to drain. Which system suits comes down to the age and size of your house:

- **Newer homes in recent subdivisions.** They hold heat well. One wall unit can heat the open plan living area. Bedrooms need separate heating.
- **Older houses on bigger sections.** They lose more heat through the ceiling and floor, and the rooms are spread out. Each room needs more capacity. Some need ducting.
- **Lifestyle properties on the outskirts.** Sleepouts and outbuildings are simpler to heat with their own small unit.

Each one is covered below. Templeton feels like a country town, but it's part of Christchurch City. So the Christchurch District Plan noise rules apply to your outdoor unit.

## Heat pump installation in newer Templeton homes

Newer Templeton homes usually have insulation, double glazing and an open plan living area. One wall unit in the right place can often heat that space. Bedrooms grouped at one end or upstairs usually need their own heating. That's either a separate small unit or a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/). If you're building, plan the pipes or ducting before the walls are lined. A new home also gives you a choice of outdoor spot. A concrete pad by a side wall, away from the bedrooms, is easier to sort before the paths and fences go in.

## Older Templeton houses and bigger sections

Older Templeton houses can be harder to heat. The insulation is thinner and the rooms are spread out. We check the ceiling and underfloor at the quote. Then we size the unit for the room it heats on a cold morning, not an average day. For a long, spread-out house, get a price on [ducted heating](/ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/) too.

## Heat pumps on semi-rural Templeton sections

On the bigger sections around Templeton, neighbours are further away. That gives us more choice of where the outdoor unit goes. We can put it on the most sheltered wall, close to the indoor unit, for a short pipe run. Got outbuildings or a sleepout? A separate small heat pump can heat that space. Then there's no need to run pipes across the section.

Rural houses can also have older switchboards. On every install, a licensed electrical worker does the electrical connection.

## Outdoor units built for Templeton's cold mornings

On clear nights, outdoor units in Templeton face frost. On those mornings the heat pump defrosts its outdoor coil every so often. That meltwater has to go somewhere. We fit units on raised feet or a bracket, clear of the ground and grass. Where we can, we pick the side of the house sheltered from the strongest wind.
