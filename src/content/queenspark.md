## Heat pump installation in Queenspark family homes

In Queenspark we size a heat pump for the house as it is now. Many of its family homes have changed since they were built. Most are single storey, brick or weatherboard, built from the 1970s on. They have garages and gardens.

- **Original windows and insulation.** These homes lose more heat through the glass and walls. The lounge needs more capacity than in a newer house the same size.
- **Double glazing and wall insulation added.** These may need a smaller heat pump than their age suggests.
- **Extended homes.** Some have the lounge opened up to the kitchen or carried through into a family room. The space to heat is bigger than the original plan shows.

Everything's on one level. So a lounge unit, plus bedroom units where you want them, can cover the whole house.

## Wall, floor or ceiling heat pumps in Queenspark

Most Queenspark living rooms suit a high wall unit. We mount it where the air can reach across the room without blowing straight at the couch. It isn't the only choice, though:

- **Floor console.** Good under a window, or in a room without much wall space up high.
- **High wall.** The most common choice. Compact and quiet.
- **Ducted.** Hidden in the ceiling with a vent in each room, if the roof space allows.

Which one suits your lounge is the main question in [choosing a heat pump](/choosing-heat-pump/).

## Open plan living and extensions in Queenspark

When a lounge has been opened to the kitchen or extended into a family room, the space is bigger than the original plan suggests. One unit can cover it. It just has to be sized for the whole area and placed centrally. If the extension sits at an angle to the original room, two smaller units may heat it more evenly.

## The outdoor unit on a Queenspark section

On Queenspark's flat sections, the outdoor unit usually goes on a pad or bracket on a side wall. We keep it out of corners. We raise it so defrost water drains. We keep it away from the neighbour's windows.

Queenspark is on the north-east side of Christchurch, close to Parklands and Burwood. Easterly winds carry some salt this far. So give the coil a light rinse from time to time.
