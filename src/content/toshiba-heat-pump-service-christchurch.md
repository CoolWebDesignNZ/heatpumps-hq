## What we check on a Toshiba service or repair

When we open up a Toshiba unit, we follow a specific checklist. It's the same process whether it's a routine service or a fault-finding call.

- **Fault codes and controller history:** We pull the diagnostic history to see if the unit has been cutting out or defrosting incorrectly.
- **Refrigerant pressure:** We test the pressure to catch slow leaks before they damage the compressor.
- **Coil and filter deep-clean:** Toshiba units are prone to airflow restriction if the coil behind the filters gets clogged with dust and pollen.
- **Electrical draw and connections:** We check the power draw to ensure the unit isn't working harder than it should.
- **Outdoor unit condition:** We check the siting, clearance, and drainage. If a Toshiba outdoor unit is boxed in or sitting on the ground, it will struggle to defrost on a frosty Christchurch morning.

## How Toshiba units handle Christchurch conditions

Christchurch weather puts heat pumps through two extremes: the dry, dusty nor'west winds and the hard, clear frosts.

- **The nor'west dust:** The nor'west wind brings fine dust that clogs indoor filters and outdoor coils quickly. If you live on the western side of the city, your Toshiba will need its filters cleaned more often than the manual suggests, or the unit will lose efficiency and start to smell.
- **Frost and defrost cycles:** Toshiba outdoor units have a defrost cycle to melt ice off the coil. But if the unit is installed in a frost pocket (like the Selwyn plains) or a damp, shaded corner, the defrost cycle runs constantly, which eats into your heating efficiency. We check the outdoor siting during every service to make sure it can drain and defrost properly.

## Sizing a Toshiba for your home

We see a lot of undersized units in older Christchurch villas and bungalows. Single glazing and minimal insulation mean the heat pump has to work much harder. Conversely, a Toshiba unit that is perfect for a draughty 1920s house will be massively oversized for a new, airtight townhouse in Halswell. We size the Toshiba from your rooms, insulation, and glazing, not just the floor plan. Sometimes that means a smaller unit than you expected.
