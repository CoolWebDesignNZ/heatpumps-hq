## Heat pump installation in Upper Riccarton rentals and units

In Upper Riccarton, the heat pump you need depends on the kind of home you have. It also depends on whether it's a rental that has to meet the heating standard. Older houses on full sections here have been split into flats. Others have made way for two or three townhouses. Student rentals sit on the same streets as family homes. So you'll find three quite different setups in one block.

- **Small units and one-bedroom flats.** One compact high wall unit in the living area does it. We size it to that room's volume and glass. A bedroom behind a closed door won't get much of its heat.
- **Infill townhouses over two levels.** These need heat upstairs too.
- **Older flats in converted houses.** They can lose heat through the ceiling and draughty doors and windows faster than a heat pump can easily put it back. We check those first.

Upper Riccarton sits between Riccarton and the university, so many of these homes are rentals.

## Meeting the heating standard in an Upper Riccarton rental

If you're a landlord in Upper Riccarton, the main living room has to meet the Healthy Homes heating standard. In short, you need a fixed heater that heats that room directly. It needs enough capacity. If it's a heat pump, it needs a thermostat. Portable heaters and open fires don't count.

We size your rental's heat pump to the living room's required capacity. We fit it where it heats that room directly. Then we give you the model details for your records.

## Heat pumps for upstairs rooms in Upper Riccarton townhouses

Warm air rises. So you'd think a downstairs heat pump would warm the bedrooms above. In practice a narrow stairwell carries very little of it. The upstairs rooms in a two storey townhouse can stay cold on a frosty night. The fix is a small wall unit upstairs. A [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/) with heads on both levels works too. If the outdoor unit goes on an upper wall, we use a bracket rated for its weight. We run the pipes neatly down the cladding.

## Older Upper Riccarton flats

Some older flats here only ever had a plug-in heater. Before we fit a heat pump, we check the ceiling insulation. We look for gaps around doors and windows too. A sealed, insulated room needs less capacity, and it's cheaper to keep warm.

## Close neighbours and outdoor units in Upper Riccarton

Infill sections leave little space between homes. The outdoor unit often ends up a few steps from next door's wall. The Christchurch District Plan limits are measured at the neighbouring site. On cold mornings, it's the night limit that counts. So we keep units off shared walls and away from the neighbours' bedroom windows. We also keep them out of tight corners, where sound bounces.
