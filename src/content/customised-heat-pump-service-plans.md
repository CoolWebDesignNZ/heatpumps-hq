## Who a heat pump service plan suits

A plan helps when keeping track of servicing is the hard part. That's usually:

- **Homes with several units.** Say a multi-room or ducted setup, plus a wall unit in the garage or sleepout. It's easy to lose track.
- **Landlords and property managers.** One schedule covers one rental or many, and you get a record of each visit.
- **Homes with allergies or asthma.** Clean coils and filters matter more when someone reacts to dust and mould. See [heat pumps for allergies and asthma](/allergy-asthma-solutions-heat-pumps/).
- **Hard-working heat pumps.** A unit heating a home office all day, or cooling bedrooms all summer, needs more attention than one used on winter evenings.
- **Holiday homes and baches.** Units that sit idle for months. They need a check before you use the place.

Got one unit and remember to book each year? You probably don't need a plan. A yearly [heat pump service](/heat-pump-servicing/) does the job.

## How we set your heat pump maintenance plan

There's no set menu. We work it out from what's in front of us.

1. **What you have.** How many indoor and outdoor units, their brands and ages, and whether any are ducted or floor console.
2. **How you use them.** Heating only, or heating and cooling, all day or just evenings. More hours means more dust through the filters.
3. **What they live with.** Pets shed into filters. Salt air off the sea eats outdoor coils. Gravel drives and paddocks get dust into everything. Kitchens get grease into nearby units.
4. **Who lives there.** Babies, older people and anyone with breathing problems are reasons to service more often.

Then we agree how often each unit gets done, and what time of year suits. A home that only heats might have one visit before winter. A home that cools all summer might add a second visit in spring.

## Heat pump maintenance plans for Christchurch homes

Where your heat pumps live in Christchurch shapes the plan.

- **Salt by the sea.** In [Redcliffs](/redcliffs/) and [Moncks Bay](/moncks-bay/), salt builds up on the outdoor coil. Rinse it gently with a hose every couple of months. The yearly clean lets us spot corrosion early.
- **Dust on the city edge.** In [Yaldhurst](/yaldhurst/), dust blows in off the paddocks and shingle driveways and clogs the coil. We book that visit before winter.
- **Summer cooling.** On hot nor'west days in [Harewood](/harewood/), a unit cooling all summer can clog its filters as fast as in winter. That's the home where a second visit in spring makes sense.
- **Student flats.** In [Ilam](/ilam/), heat pumps in shared flats run long hours and get little care. We can book a yearly service before each academic year starts.
- **Between tenancies.** In [Aranui](/aranui/), a rental heat pump gets used hard and often gets little care between tenants. A service between tenancies heads off most repairs.

## What a heat pump maintenance contract costs

The quote for each visit is set by the same things as any service: how many units, what type of system, how easy each one is to get at, and what shape it's in. If we find a part that needs replacing, we price it separately. Nothing gets done without your OK.

## What each planned visit includes

Every visit follows our full service checklist, from the filters, indoor coil, fan wheel and drain to the outdoor coil, base and electrical connections. Then we test run it in heating and cooling. We go through each step under [heat pump servicing](/heat-pump-servicing/).

After each visit you get a short note for each unit. It says what we did and anything we'd keep an eye on. Over a few years, that builds into a handy record of how each unit is ageing.

## Service plans for landlords

Landlords are responsible for maintaining heaters and ventilation. Tenants must keep the filters they can reach reasonably clean. If the job needs know-how or tools, or the filters are hard to reach, the landlord generally has to do it.

A plan gives you a dated record for each unit. That's handy when tenants change, or when a question about heating comes up. We can sort out access with the tenant ourselves, on a weekday between 8am and 5pm.

## Looking after the heat pump between planned visits

You still need to wash the filters every few weeks when it's working hard. Keep a clear space round the outdoor unit too. The jobs you can do yourself are under [heat pump maintenance](/maintenance-servicing/).
