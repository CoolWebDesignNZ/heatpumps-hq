## Heat pump installation for Cracroft hill homes

Cracroft sits on the Port Hills, on the slopes beside Cashmere. Sections are steep, homes are often built into the slope over two or more levels, and some sit well above or below the road. The outlook across the city shapes how many of the houses are designed, with living rooms facing the view and glass to match.

Every heat pump install here starts with the site rather than the unit.

## Getting the gear to a Cracroft house

Outdoor units are bulky and heavy. On a Cracroft section the route from the van matters: long flights of steps, narrow paths along the side of the house, decks with drops. We walk it at the quote visit. It affects how many people the job needs and occasionally which unit is practical.

The same route matters for every service afterwards. A unit that can only be reached by ladder over a drop is harder to look after, so we choose a position that stays accessible.

## Mounting a heat pump on a Cracroft slope

Flat, firm ground beside a hill home is scarce. The usual options:

- **A galvanised bracket on a solid wall**, high enough to clear the ground and let water drain.
- **A deck**, if the framing can carry the weight and vibration. Anti-vibration mounts stop hum reaching the rooms inside.
- **A small concrete pad** cut into the bank, where there's room for one and a path to reach it.

## Defrost water and wind on the hill

Hillside installs need a plan for defrost water. Left to drip, it can wear a channel in a bank or collect behind a retaining wall. We pipe it somewhere safe.

Exposure is the other factor. High, open spots take more wind and grit, so we look for a wall that offers shelter while keeping clear airflow in front of the coil. Brackets and fixings are checked at each [service](/maintenance-servicing/).

## Heat pumps for split-level rooms in Cracroft

Split-level layouts often mean the living area and bedrooms are on different floors with stairs between. A unit for the living level and a small, quiet one for the bedroom level works in a lot of these homes. We size each from its own room, including the glass.
