## Heat pump installation for Islington homes

Islington sits on the western edge of the city, between Hornby and Templeton. It is flat, and the homes sit beside farmland and industrial land. The housing is a mix of older weatherboard homes, homes from the middle of last century and some newer builds.

- **Older weatherboard and state houses.** Timber floors, single glazing and patchy insulation are common. They lose heat quickly and need more capacity than their floor area suggests. A high wall unit in the living area is the usual starting point.
- **Newer townhouses and infill homes.** These hold heat well. One unit often heats an open plan living space. The harder part is outside, where the outdoor unit has to fit a narrow side yard with clear air around it.
- **Larger family homes.** Where the living area, hallway and bedrooms all need heat, a multi-room or ducted system can be a better fit than several separate units.

## Heat pumps Islington: sizing and placement

The work on an Islington install is mostly about sizing and placement. The flat ground makes outdoor unit placement easier than on the hills. What matters is clear airflow, a spot out of the worst of the wind, and a pipe run that doesn't cross a doorway or a deck you use.

Frost is the other factor. Christchurch mornings drop below freezing often through winter, and Islington is no different. A standard heat pump handles that. If your section sits in a cold pocket, a cold climate model holds its output better when the temperature drops.

We size every install to the room and the house, not to a rule of thumb. That covers the glazing, the insulation, the ceiling height and which way the room faces.

## Heat pump service Islington: what a yearly visit checks

A yearly service keeps a heat pump running at its best through winter. It checks the filters, the indoor coil, the outdoor coil and the condensate drain. It also picks up wear, like a drain that's starting to block, before it turns into a breakdown in July.

- **Servicing.** A yearly [service](/heat-pump-servicing/) on any major brand.
- **Cleaning.** A deeper [clean](/heat-pump-cleaning/) for units that have gone a few years without one, or that smell when they start up.
- **Plans.** A [customised service plan](/customised-heat-pump-service-plans/) if you'd rather not think about booking each year.

## Heat pump repair Islington: frost, ice and faults

Islington gets the same hard frosts as the rest of the city's south west. An outdoor unit iced up on a cold morning is normal for a short defrost cycle. It should clear itself within a few minutes and go back to heating.

If it stays iced, or the unit stops heating altogether, that's worth a look. Common causes are a blocked drain, a sensor fault or low refrigerant. We work through the fault with the [troubleshooting guide](/heat-pump-problems-troubleshooting-guide/) as a starting point, then come out to diagnose it properly.

We find and repair faults on Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, Fujitsu, Panasonic and Toshiba units. If your heat pump has stopped and you need it going, see [emergency repair](/emergency-heat-pump-repair/). We take repair jobs in Islington on weekdays.
