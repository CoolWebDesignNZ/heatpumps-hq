## Heat pumps for Yaldhurst lifestyle blocks and larger homes

A Yaldhurst home needs two things from a heat pump. It has to be sized for hard frosts on still, clear nights. And the outdoor unit has to sit out of the dry nor'west. Past the edge of the suburbs, nothing stands between these houses and the weather coming off the plains. That exposure drives both choices.

- **Lifestyle block houses and bigger homes on large sections.** These are often bigger than a city home. Living areas and bedrooms are spread out. One wall unit won't reach them all. They suit [ducted heating](/ducted-heat-pumps-christchurch/) if the roof space allows. Otherwise, two or three wall units go in the rooms you use every day.
- **Homes in the newer subdivisions closer to town.** They're better insulated and more compact. A high wall unit in the open plan living area usually does it, with a smaller one for the bedrooms. Neighbours are close enough here that boundary noise counts.

At the quote we size every unit for the cold end of the range. We look at how exposed each wall is. Then we find an outdoor spot with shelter and somewhere for the defrost water to go.

## Heat pump installation sized for Yaldhurst frosts

A heat pump pulls heat from the outside air and moves it indoors. The colder it is outside, the less heat it can deliver. That's the opposite of what you need on a frosty morning. For an exposed house, we also look at units built to hold their output in the cold. See [cold climate heat pumps](/cold-climate-heat-pumps/).

A ducted system puts a vent in each room. The indoor unit sits out of sight up in the roof. It suits a big Yaldhurst home that's lived in from end to end. If you only use a few rooms every day, wall units in those rooms cost less to run.

## Where the outdoor unit goes on an open Yaldhurst section

On an open section, the outdoor unit needs shelter from the prevailing wind. It needs a raised base, clear of frost and long grass. And the defrost water has to drain somewhere it won't freeze into a sheet of ice on a path. On a rural block, we can usually find a spot well away from the neighbours. In the subdivisions, the Christchurch District Plan noise limits at the neighbour's site apply, just as they do across the city. The [boundary rules guide](/heat-pump-boundary-rules-consent-nz/) has the detail.

## Heat pumps for Yaldhurst sleepouts and studios

Lifestyle properties often have a sleepout, a studio or a home office in a converted garage. Each one can have its own compact heat pump, on its own outdoor unit, sized for that one room. That's usually simpler than running a system out from the house.

## Dust and heat pump service in Yaldhurst

Dust blows in off the paddocks and shingle driveways. It builds up on the outdoor coil and the indoor filters. A clogged coil cuts heat output just when you need it most. Book a yearly [service](/maintenance-servicing/) before winter to keep a rural heat pump running properly.
