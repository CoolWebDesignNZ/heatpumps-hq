## Avondale homes today

Avondale lies along the Avon River in the east. Land close to the river became residential red zone after the earthquakes, so some streets now have open land where houses used to be. Away from the river, the suburb is mostly mid-century homes on piles, many repaired after the quakes and some replaced with new houses.

## Renovations are the best time

A lot of Avondale homes are still being upgraded, one room or one project at a time. If you're renovating, that's the moment to plan heating. With linings off, pipes and cables can run inside walls instead of along the outside, and ducting can go into the ceiling before it's closed in. A ducted system that would be awkward to retrofit becomes much simpler. Our [ducted vs split](/ducted-vs-split-heat-pumps-christchurch/) page sets out how they compare.

Renovations also change how much heat the house needs. New insulation and double glazing can drop the size of unit required. Tell us what's planned and we'll size for the finished house.

## Original mid-century houses

In an Avondale house that hasn't been renovated, the lounge is often a separate room with single glazing. A high wall unit placed to blow down the length of the room heats it best. Bedrooms off a hall need their own unit, or a [multi-room system](/multi-room-heat-pump-installation/), to be warm at night.

## Rebuilt homes

A new Avondale house built since the earthquakes is a different job. With modern insulation and double glazing, an open plan living area can often be heated by one modest wall unit, and the bedrooms stay warmer on their own. If the house has good ceiling space, ducting can be added without walls or ceilings being opened up much.

## Outdoor units near the river

Ground near the river can stay wet and has moved in the past. We set outdoor units on a wall bracket or a firm, raised base, with enough flexibility in the pipe run to cope with small movements. The unit needs room to breathe and a clear path for defrost water.

Avondale is a few kilometres from the coast. An easterly brings some salt, so give the outdoor coil a gentle rinse now and then.

## Servicing and repairs

A heat pump fitted during earthquake repairs may not have been serviced since. A service cleans the filters, coil and fan and checks the drain and output. Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, Fujitsu, Toshiba and Panasonic units are all covered, along with most other brands. Our [servicing page](/maintenance-servicing/) explains the visit, and [repairs](/heat-pump-repair/) covers faults.
